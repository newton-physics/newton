Speed up USD import of instanced scenes by sharing mesh geometry, materials, and collision approximations across instances of one USD prototype. `ModelBuilder.approximate_meshes()` now gives shapes that share a source `Mesh` one shared replacement. Imported or approximated shapes can therefore share a `Mesh` object; replace `builder.shape_source[i]` with `builder.shape_source[i].copy()` before modifying one shape's mesh in place.
