Fix `SolverMuJoCo` compiling zero-mass bodies with mass that MuJoCo inferred from their shapes at its own default density, which hid MuJoCo's check that movable bodies have mass. A movable body without mass or inertia, and without a rigidly attached body that has them, now raises `ValueError` at solver construction instead of simulating with NaNs; this includes kinematic roots, which previously ran only when they had a shape.
