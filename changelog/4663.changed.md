Default `ModelBuilder.add_urdf(joint_damping_mode="passive")` to import URDF damping into `joint_damping`, keeping drive gains independent. Remove the deprecated `None` mode and its transition warning; omit the argument or pass `"passive"` for passive damping. Pass `joint_damping_mode="drive"` to retain the Newton 1.6 mapping into `joint_target_kd`. Both explicit modes remain supported. XPBD and VBD currently do not consume passive `joint_damping`; use drive mode or configure controller drive gains explicitly to retain their former imported damping.
