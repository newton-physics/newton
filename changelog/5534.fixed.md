Fix `SolverXPBD` hinges about the joint frame's Y or Z axis (revolute joints and D6 joints with one angular axis), such as MJCF hinges with `axis="0 0 1"`: at large joint angles, position targets could spin the joint up instead of reaching the target, and driven chains could leave the hinge plane or turn non-finite. These hinges now move like the same hinge about the frame's X axis. Hinge axes that are not a frame axis now keep their limits and drives on the hinge angle.
