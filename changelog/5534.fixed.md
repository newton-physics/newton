Fix `SolverXPBD` hinges whose axis is not along the joint frame's X axis (revolute joints and D6 joints with one angular axis), such as MJCF hinges with `axis="0 0 1"`: at large joint angles, position targets could spin the joint up instead of reaching the target, and driven chains could leave the hinge plane or turn non-finite. Hinges about the frame's Y or Z axis now move like the same hinge about X, and other axes keep their limits and drives on the hinge angle.
