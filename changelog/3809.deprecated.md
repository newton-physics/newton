Deprecate the earlier AOUSD surface material attributes `physics:thickness`, `physics:stretchStiffness`, `physics:shearStiffness`, and `physics:bendStiffness`. They remain accepted with their former modulus interpretation during the deprecation window. To preserve behavior, migrate `physics:thickness` to `physics:surfaceThickness`, multiply stretch/shear moduli by thickness and bend modulus by thickness cubed, and author the converted values as structural `physics:surface*Stiffness` attributes.
