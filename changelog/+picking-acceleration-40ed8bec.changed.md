Change viewer mouse-picking to command an acceleration of the pick point instead of applying a mass-heuristic force. `pick_stiffness` [1/s²] and `pick_damping` [1/s] are now mass-independent, and their defaults rise to 500 and 45 so a held body sags about 2 cm instead of 20 cm. The commanded acceleration is scaled by the operational-space mass at the pick point, computed from the articulation Jacobian and joint-space mass matrix, which keeps light distal links such as feet and fingertips responsive. Bodies connected by rod joints use the total mass of their articulation, since no compatible Jacobian is available for them. The per-scene gain overrides have been removed from the examples. Retune custom `Picking` gains that were tuned for the previous formula.
