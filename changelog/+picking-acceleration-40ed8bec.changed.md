Change viewer mouse-picking to command an acceleration of the pick point instead of applying a mass-heuristic force. `pick_stiffness` [1/s²] and `pick_damping` [1/s] are now mass-independent. The commanded acceleration is scaled by the operational-space mass at the pick point, computed from the articulation Jacobian and joint-space mass matrix and bounded below by `pick_min_inertia_fraction` of the articulation's total mass. The picked body's own weight is cancelled. The weight cancellation makes light free bodies controllable. The bounded operational-space mass does the same for light distal links such as feet and fingertips. The per-scene gain overrides have been removed from the examples. Bodies connected by cable joints use the articulation's total mass, since no Jacobian is available for them. Retune custom `Picking` gains that were tuned for the previous formula; the defaults are unchanged.
