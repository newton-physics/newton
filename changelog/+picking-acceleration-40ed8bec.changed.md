Change viewer mouse-picking to command an acceleration of the pick point instead of applying a mass-heuristic force. `pick_stiffness` [1/s²] and `pick_damping` [1/s] are now mass-independent, and their defaults rise to 500 and 45 so a held body sags about 2 cm instead of 20 cm. The commanded acceleration is mapped to a force through the operational-space inertia at the pick point, computed from the articulation Jacobian and joint-space mass matrix, which keeps light distal links such as feet and fingertips responsive. Bodies connected by rod joints use the total mass of their articulation, since each six-DOF rod joint would otherwise resolve only a single segment's mass. The per-scene gain overrides have been removed from the examples. Retune gain overrides for the previous formula and set them through the `pick_stiffness` and `pick_damping` properties of `newton.viewer.Picking`, for example on `viewer.picking`, or drop the override to use the new defaults.
