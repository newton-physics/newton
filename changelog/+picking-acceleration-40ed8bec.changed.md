Change viewer mouse-picking to command an acceleration of the pick point instead of applying a mass-heuristic force. `pick_stiffness` [1/s²] and `pick_damping` [1/s] are now mass-independent. The commanded acceleration is scaled by the operational-space mass at the pick point, computed from the articulation Jacobian and joint-space mass matrix and bounded below by `pick_min_inertia_fraction` of the articulation's total mass. The picked body's own weight is cancelled. Light distal links such as feet and fingertips are now as easy to drag as the base, and the per-scene gain overrides have been removed from the examples. Bodies connected by cable joints use the articulation's total mass, since no Jacobian is available for them. Retune custom `Picking` gains that were tuned for the previous formula; the defaults are unchanged.
