Change viewer mouse-picking to command an acceleration of the pick point instead of a mass-heuristic force; picking gains are now mass-independent (`pick_stiffness` [1/s²], `pick_damping` [1/s]). The commanded acceleration is converted to a force using the operational-space mass at the pick point, derived from the articulation Jacobian and joint-space mass matrix, so grabbing a light distal link such as a foot or fingertip is as effective as grabbing the base. Retune custom `Picking` gains that were tuned for the previous force-based formula; the defaults are unchanged.
