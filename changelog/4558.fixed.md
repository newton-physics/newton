Fix contact-indexed solver observables when a separate collision pipeline shares a model with `SolverCoupledADMM`. Contact fields are now sized from the `Contacts` passed to `solver.observables()` instead of freezing the model's capacities, and `validate_observables()` and `SensorContact.update()` accept any `Contacts` with the same device and capacities as the allocation instead of requiring the identical instance.
