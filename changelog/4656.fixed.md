Fix `SolverMuJoCo` resolving `mujoco:pair_*` rows to the wrong world when a model mixes global and per-world rows, or when a row is skipped during conversion. Pair properties now resolve through a `mjc_pair_to_newton_pair` mapping that records the Newton row each compiled pair came from, so template-world rows are read per world and global rows (`pair_world` < 0) are read by every world at their absolute index. Rows owned by a single world are now rejected by the separate-worlds homogeneity check instead of being silently dropped for the other worlds; author them with `pair_world = -1` to share them.
