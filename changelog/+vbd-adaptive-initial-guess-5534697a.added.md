Add `particle_initial_guess="adaptive"` to `SolverVBD`, which starts each step from the adaptive initial guess of the VBD paper instead of the inertial target. With a fixed iteration count, the inertial guess leaves damped (`k_damp`) soft bodies resting with too much sag. The adaptive guess rests them at the converged shape. Connected bodies that nothing holds (no contact, fixed particle or contact with another body) start at the inertial target, so free fall stays exact, stiff bodies included. The default stays `"inertia"`.
