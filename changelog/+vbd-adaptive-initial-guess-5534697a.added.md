Add `particle_initial_guess="adaptive"` to `SolverVBD`, which starts each step from the adaptive initial guess of the VBD paper instead of the inertial target. With a fixed iteration count, the inertial guess leaves damped (`k_damp`) soft bodies resting with too much sag. The adaptive guess rests them at the converged shape and keeps free fall unchanged. The default stays `"inertia"`.
