Sort soft-contact candidate pairs by shape on every device. The contact set is unchanged, but records are emitted in a different order, so CPU and deterministic-mode results can shift within floating-point tolerance. The ordering favors scenes whose candidate sets are much larger than their active contacts (for example many replicated worlds); in small dense-contact scenes against a few same-type shapes the edge/face generation pass can be modestly slower (within 1 to 2 percent of a full step).
