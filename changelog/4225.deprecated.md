Deprecate passing optional arguments positionally to `ModelBuilder.add_particle()`, `ModelBuilder.add_particles()`, `ModelBuilder.add_spring()`, and `ModelBuilder.add_tetrahedron()`. Positional use still works and emits a `NewtonDeprecationWarning`. Pass `radius`, `flags`, `custom_attributes`, `k_mu`, `k_lambda`, and `k_damp` by keyword, as for the other `ModelBuilder.add_*()` methods.
