Speed up the FeatherPGS per-step contact coloring and friction-patch build on CUDA. The `propagation-colored` coloring pass now stages its serial passes and sort keys in shared memory, and friction regions grow with one warp per body pair instead of one thread. Colors, row order and friction patches are unchanged. On a 60-body single-world pile the two passes drop from 4.0 to 1.9 ms per step.
