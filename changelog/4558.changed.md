Size contact-indexed solver observables from the `Contacts` passed to `SolverBase.observables()`, or the solver-owned pipeline's contacts, instead of the model's published capacity. Steps, `SensorContact`, and the viewer now accept any compatible `Contacts` on the solver device whose capacities fit the allocated arrays, and check that contact forces are consumed with the buffer that produced them. Allocate observables from the largest buffer they will be used with; a `CollisionPipeline` no longer needs to exist before allocation.
