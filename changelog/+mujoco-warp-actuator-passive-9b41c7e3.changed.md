Apply MuJoCo actuator armature on the MuJoCo Warp backend of `SolverMuJoCo`, which previously ignored it with a `NewtonWarning`, and accept per-world and runtime `mujoco:actuator_gear` edits on actuators with passive damping, which previously raised `ValueError`. `mjw_model.dof_damping` and `mjw_model.tendon_damping` no longer include actuator damping; read `mjw_model.actuator_damping` and `mjw_model.actuator_dampingpoly` instead.
