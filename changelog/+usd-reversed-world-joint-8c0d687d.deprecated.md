Deprecate importing a D6 joint, or single-axis joints merged into one D6 joint, in `ModelBuilder.add_usd()` when its `physics:body1` is the world or a static prim and it has more than one rotational axis, or a translational axis that is not parallel to its rotational axis. Newton cannot reverse the motion of these joints, so they keep their authored axes as before, their limits, drives, and initial state may act in the mirrored direction, and the import emits a `NewtonDeprecationWarning`. A future release will raise an error. Author the joint with the rigid body as `physics:body1` instead.
