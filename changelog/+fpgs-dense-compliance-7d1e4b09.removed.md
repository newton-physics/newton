Remove the `SolverFeatherPGS` parameters `dense_contact_compliance` and `speculative_dense_contact_compliance`. They only enlarged the dense Gauss-Seidel divisor without changing the converged contact solution. Passing them now raises `TypeError`; the constructor order after `pgs_cfm` is `pgs_omega, pgs_contact_regularization`. `pgs_contact_regularization` is the remaining contact regularizer and applies to dense rows as well.
