Add MuJoCo flex support to `SolverMuJoCo`. Springs, triangles, and tetrahedra become 1D, 2D, and 3D flexes whose vertices are synchronized with `State.particle_q` and `State.particle_qd`. Use `newton.solvers.mujoco.add_flex()` to author individual flexes with their own material, elasticity, self-collision, and contact settings.
