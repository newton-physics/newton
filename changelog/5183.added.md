Add MuJoCo flex support to `SolverMuJoCo`. Springs, triangles, and tetrahedra become 1D, 2D, and 3D flexes whose vertices are synchronized with `State.particle_q` and `State.particle_qd` and driven by `State.particle_f`, and flexes collide with each other and with particle-colliding shapes. Use `newton.solvers.mujoco.add_flex()` to author individual flexes with their own material, elasticity, edge equality constraints, self-collision, and contact settings. See the `mujoco_flex` example.
