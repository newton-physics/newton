Remove `ModelBuilder.add_rod(positions=...)`, `ModelBuilder.add_rod_graph()`, `newton.utils.CableStiffness`, and the four `newton.utils.create_*` cable helpers, deprecated in 1.6. Construct `newton.Rod(points, ...)` (with `edges=...` for a graph), supply geometry and material properties on the Rod, and assemble it with `ModelBuilder.add_rod(rod=...)`. Use `Rod.create_straight()` for straight geometry and `Rod.quaternions` for segment frames; call `Rod.compute_frames(twist_total=...)` first for nonzero twist.
