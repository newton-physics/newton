Drop the dedicated error of the deprecated `Model.mujoco.dof_springref` on models where it cannot alias `Model.joint_rest_q` (joints with more coordinates than DOFs, or nonzero `mujoco:dof_ref`). The name no longer exists there: reading it raises a plain `AttributeError`, and assigning it is no longer rejected and has no effect. Use `Model.joint_rest_q` (`springref - ref`, indexed by `joint_q_start`).
