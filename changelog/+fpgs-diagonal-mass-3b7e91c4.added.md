`SolverFeatherPGS` now detects articulations whose degrees of freedom are not coupled, such as independent single-DOF branches of a fixed base, and solves their structurally diagonal mass matrix by per-DOF division instead of a factorization, with results bitwise identical to the dense `loop` kernels and equal to the `tiled` kernels up to float32 rounding. The selection is structural and takes precedence over sparse mass factors.
