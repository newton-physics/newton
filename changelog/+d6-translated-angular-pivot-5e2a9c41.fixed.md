Fix D6 joints with both linear and angular axes so that their angular axes pivot about the joint origin translated by the linear DOFs, matching `eval_fk`. This affects `SolverFeatherstone`, `SolverSteel`, `eval_jacobian`, `eval_mass_matrix`, `eval_inverse_dynamics_passive` and the analytic and mixed Jacobians of `IKSolver`. Such articulations could gain energy and become unstable once they moved away from the joint anchor.
