Add experimental opt-in dry joint friction to ``SolverSteel``: ``enable_joint_friction=True`` enforces ``Model.joint_friction`` of PRISMATIC, REVOLUTE and D6 DOFs as a per-DOF impulse box in the matrix-free solve. Joint DOF notifications check the coefficients on the device, also when captured in a CUDA graph, flag invalid ones in ``SolverSteel.joint_friction_invalid`` and report them through ``SolverSteel.check_joint_friction``.
