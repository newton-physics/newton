Deprecate `JointType.CABLE` and `ModelBuilder.add_joint_cable()` in favor of `JointType.ROD` and `ModelBuilder.add_joint_rod()`. The old names remain functional through 1.6 and are eligible for removal in 1.7. Code that persists enum names should accept both `"CABLE"` and `"ROD"` while migrating and write `"ROD"` explicitly.
