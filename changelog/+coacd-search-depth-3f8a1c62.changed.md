Search two cut levels deep (`mcts_max_depth=2`, `mcts_iterations=20`) in CoACD convex decomposition from `ModelBuilder.approximate_meshes(method="coacd")` and USD `convexDecomposition` imports. This produces about a third as many convex parts with the same mean volume accuracy, making collision cheaper, at roughly 1.8x the decomposition time. The resulting parts are larger, so on dense, smooth meshes the `Mesh.maxhullvert` cap (64 by default) trims more of each part and can leave more of the surface uncovered; raise the source mesh's `maxhullvert` to keep such details. To restore the previous decompositions, pass `mcts_iterations=5, mcts_max_depth=1` to `approximate_meshes()`. `add_usd()` does not accept CoACD settings, so for USD imports pass `skip_mesh_approximation=True` and then call `approximate_meshes(method="coacd", shape_indices=..., mcts_iterations=5, mcts_max_depth=1)` on the shapes to decompose.
