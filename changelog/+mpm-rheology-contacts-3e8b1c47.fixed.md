Fix `SolverImplicitMPM.Config.collider_normal_from_sdf_gradient` disabling the `"pic"` and `"picN"` particle contacts, which let material fall through colliders. Collider normals now come from a quadratic B-spline reconstruction of the collider signed distance sampled at the grid cell vertices, which turns smoothly around collider edges and seams; vertices behind a contact's tangent plane take their depth below that plane, so the reconstructed distance never exceeds the exact one, also across walls thinner than a cell. Particle contacts also take their gap from this reconstruction.
