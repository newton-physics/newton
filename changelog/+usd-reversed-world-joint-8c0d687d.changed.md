Warn in `ModelBuilder.add_usd()` for a D6 joint, or single-axis joints merged into one D6 joint, whose `physics:body1` is the world or a static prim and that has more than one rotational axis, or a translational axis that is not parallel to its rotational axis. Newton cannot reverse the motion of these joints, so they keep their authored axes as before, and any limits, drives, or initial state may act in the mirrored direction. Author the joint with the rigid body as `physics:body1` to avoid this.
