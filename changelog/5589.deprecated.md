Deprecate passing a `bool` as `deterministic` to `CollisionPipeline`, `geometry.NarrowPhase` and `geometry.HydroelasticSDF` in Newton 1.9. Pass `wp.DeterministicMode.RUN_TO_RUN` instead of `True` and `wp.DeterministicMode.NOT_GUARANTEED` instead of `False`. Boolean values keep working with a `NewtonDeprecationWarning` for at least one full minor release cycle.
