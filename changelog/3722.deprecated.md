Deprecate the earlier AOUSD cable material attributes `physics:thickness`, `physics:stretchStiffness`, `physics:shearStiffness`, `physics:bendStiffness`, and `physics:twistStiffness`. They remain accepted with their former modulus interpretation during the deprecation window. To preserve behavior, migrate `physics:thickness` to `physics:curvesThickness` and author all four structural modes: multiply explicitly authored stretch/shear by cross-section area and bend/twist by the second/polar moments; where legacy shear or twist was omitted, copy the converted stretch or bend value, respectively. Re-test unevenly sampled cables that omitted legacy stretch stiffness: their former nonzero per-joint default cannot be represented by one material-level structural value.
