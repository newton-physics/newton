Split `SolverSteel`'s per-step kinematics and inverse-dynamics pass: the serial per-articulation kernel now stops at the poses, motion subspaces, velocities and accelerations, and a one-thread-per-body kernel forms the spatial inertias and bias wrenches and publishes the COM velocities, the same arithmetic in parallel. On Franka Lift at 393,216 worlds the fused kernel was the largest single kernel of the step.
