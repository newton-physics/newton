Add automatically selected mass-matrix paths to `SolverFeatherPGS`: sparse factors in the fill-free pattern of the kinematic tree for branched articulations of one shared topology, and per-DOF division for articulations whose degrees of freedom are not coupled (bitwise identical to the dense `loop` kernels). The selection is structural, not a performance prediction, and sparse factors can be markedly slower on some topologies. Add the opt-in `parallel_tree` option, which traverses the independent branches of each articulation tree in parallel on CUDA.
