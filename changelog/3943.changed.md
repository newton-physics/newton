Keep matched rigid-soft contact points in `CollisionPipeline(contact_matching="sticky")`: `Contacts.soft_contact_body_pos` and `Contacts.soft_contact_barycentric` hold their previous values while the points touch, have slipped apart by at most `contact_matching_pos_threshold`, and lie on a surface without surface velocity. Each `Contacts` buffer replays its own records, as its consumers left them; `SolverVBD` drags the kept body points along once a contact slides. Only `SolverVBD` measures friction from them; other solvers read these points as before, so their contact point may lag the closest point by up to the threshold. Use `contact_matching="latest"` to keep fresh rigid-soft points.
