Add independent initial and structural-rest poses through the `rest_points`, `rest_quaternions`, and `rest_straight` inputs on `Rod`. Use `Rod(..., rest_straight=True)` to remove intrinsic bend and twist from an ordered chain without changing its initial pose or segment lengths, then assemble it with `ModelBuilder.add_rod(rod=rod)`. For individual joints, use `ModelBuilder.add_joint_rod(rest_rotation=...)`. Rod angular rest is stored in `Model.joint_target_q` independently of `Control.joint_target_q`; after modifying those Model-owned rest targets, call `SolverVBD.notify_model_changed(ModelFlags.JOINT_PROPERTIES)`.
