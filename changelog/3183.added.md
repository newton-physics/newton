Add independent initial and structural-rest poses for experimental rods and Rod joints through the `rest_positions` and `rest_quaternions` arguments of `ModelBuilder.add_rod()` and the `rest_rotation` argument of `ModelBuilder.add_joint_rod()`. Set `rest_straight=True` on `ModelBuilder.add_rod()` to remove intrinsic bend and twist from an ordered chain without changing its initial pose or segment lengths. Rod angular rest is stored in `Model.joint_target_q`, independently of `Control.joint_target_q`; after modifying `Model.joint_target_q`, call `SolverVBD.notify_model_changed(ModelFlags.JOINT_PROPERTIES)`.
