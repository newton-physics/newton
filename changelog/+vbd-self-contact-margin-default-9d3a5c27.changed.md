Interpret `SolverVBD(particle_self_contact_margin=...)` without `particle_self_contact_gap` as the self-contact interaction distance with a zero gap, completing the migration announced in Newton 1.6. The margin was previously the detection query radius with a fixed interaction distance of 0.2 m, and emitted a `NewtonDeprecationWarning`; margins below 0.2 m with self-contact enabled no longer raise `ValueError`. To keep the previous distances for a margin `m`, pass `particle_self_contact_margin=0.2, particle_self_contact_gap=m - 0.2`. The deprecated `particle_self_contact_radius` still selects the legacy radius and margin pair.
