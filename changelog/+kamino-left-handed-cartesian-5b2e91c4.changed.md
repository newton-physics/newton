Raise `ValueError` in `SolverKamino` for a joint with three translational axes that form a left-handed set, such as the negated axes of a USD D6 joint whose `physics:body1` is the world. Kamino previously simulated these joints incorrectly, letting the body rotate even though all rotations were locked. Use another solver for such joints, or author the D6 joint with the rigid body as `physics:body1`.
