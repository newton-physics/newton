Apply `SolverFeatherPGS` `pgs_contact_regularization` on every contact route (matrix-free, dense, propagation) instead of matrix-free rows only, and solve positive-gap speculative rows and rows whose rebound target fires rigid so restitution is `e * v_in` at any `g`. The regularizer is documented as a numerical damped compliance: it makes indeterminate normal-force splits unique and holds stacks that the exact rigid law drops at the same iteration count, at a resting sag of `g * a * dt^2 / pgs_beta` per loaded row. Values above 1e6 are rejected as numerically ineffective in the float32 solve. At the exact-rigid weight, per-row weight buffers collapse to one shared element, producer stores and consumer reads are skipped, and no extra kernel runs.
