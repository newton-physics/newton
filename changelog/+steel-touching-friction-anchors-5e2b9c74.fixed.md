Fix `SolverSteel` friction patches placing their anchors on separated contacts of a body pair that also touches. Such anchors applied the patch's whole friction budget at points that do not touch, so a small part pinched by a gripper could slide and spin in the grasp. Once a body pair touches, only contacts within 1% of the smaller body's radius of touching now hold friction anchors; a pair that is only speculatively in contact keeps its nearest contacts.
