Add experimental opt-in passive-island sleeping to FeatherPGS with device-side connectivity, wake propagation, and omission of dormant contact, joint-limit and velocity-limit rows. With `sleep_skip_constraints`, islands that sleep through a step also skip their articulated dynamics (kinematics, inverse dynamics, drives, mass matrix and factorization, triangular solves, velocity prediction and integration) and publish their frozen state; a woken articulation refreshes its mass matrix on its first awake step. Collision still runs, and sleeping stays disabled by default. Sleeping requires `articulated_contact_response="immediate"` and does not compose with `propagation-colored`. It composes with persistent friction anchors and contact torsion; articulations that own mimic or loop constraints stay awake, and warmstarting, compliance and velocity passes remain unsupported.
