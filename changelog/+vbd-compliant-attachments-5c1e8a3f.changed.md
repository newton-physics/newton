Solve particle-body attachments in `SolverVBD` as compliant augmented-Lagrangian rows, like structural joints, so very stiff attachments hold their anchors closely without ill-conditioned penalties. Results for soft attachments change slightly within the iteration budget. If loaded anchors drift, raise `SolverVBD` iterations or reduce the time step, or use `stiffness=math.inf` for a hard point constraint.
