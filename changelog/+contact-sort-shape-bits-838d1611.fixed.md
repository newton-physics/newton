Fix deterministic contact ordering and contact matching for models with more than `2**20` shapes, whose shape indices aliased in the contact sort key. Models with only primitive and convex shapes fit up to `2**30` shapes in one key. Larger models, including models with meshes, heightfields, hydroelastic shapes or primitive shapes with an SDF above `2**20` shapes, sort by a separate shape-pair key in a second stable radix pass. Resolve competing contact claims by sorted row index so contacts with identical fingerprints cannot claim the same previous contact. A standalone deterministic `NarrowPhase` needs both `shape_aabb_lower` and `shape_aabb_upper` at construction to support more than `2**20` shapes; without them it still raises `ValueError` at launch above that limit.
