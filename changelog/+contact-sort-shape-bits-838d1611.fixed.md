Fix deterministic contact ordering and contact matching for models with more than `2**20` shapes, whose shape indices aliased in the contact sort key. Models with only primitive and convex shapes now fit up to `2**30` shapes. Models with meshes, heightfields or hydroelastic shapes raise `ValueError` at construction above `2**20` shapes when `deterministic=True` or contact matching is enabled, and a deterministic `NarrowPhase` built without shape AABBs raises at launch above `2**20` shapes.
