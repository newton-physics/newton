Add `particle_contact_ke` to `SolverVBD`: a contact stiffness per particle in place of the model-wide `soft_contact_ke` on the particle side. A self-contact pair uses the harmonic mean of its two sides and a particle-shape contact averages the particle's value with the shape's, so a soft body keeps its own contact spring when a much stiffer body shares the scene. With one model-wide value set for the stiff body, a soft body's own near-surface contacts could get a spring a hundred times too stiff and blow apart, even far from the stiff body. The default keeps the model-wide value.
