Remove the error guard left behind for `Actuator.controller`, `Actuator.State.controller_state`, `ActuatorParsed.controller_class`, and `ActuatorParsed.controller_kwargs`, which were removed in Newton 1.8. Reading them now raises a plain `AttributeError`, and assigning them is no longer rejected: the assignment creates an unused attribute. Use `drive`, `drive_state`, `drive_class`, and `drive_kwargs`.
