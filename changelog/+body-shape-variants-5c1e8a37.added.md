Add experimental per-world body shape variants: declare alternative shape sets with `ModelBuilder.add_body_variants()`, select them per world at runtime with `Model.select_body_variants()`, which also sets the body's mass properties, query them with `Model.body_variant` and `Model.body_variant_labels()`, and propagate the change with the new `ModelFlags.SHAPE_TYPE_AND_SOURCE` flag and `ViewerBase.notify_model_changed()`.
