Trace shapes, standalone particles and deformable surfaces in one traversal of a per-camera acceleration structure, which `SensorCamera.update()` and `SensorCamera.sync_deformable_meshes()` refit from the state. The camera no longer reads `Model.bvh_particles`, so it no longer needs `Model.bvh_refit_particles()`. A camera with particles or deformable surfaces takes the shape bounds of the last `Model.bvh_refit_shapes()` when it syncs, so after moving shapes, sync it (the default of `update()`) rather than passing `sync_deformables=False`.
