Add `CouplingInterface.coupling_effective_mass_refresh_flags()`, which names the `ModelFlags` after which `SolverCoupledProxy` and `SolverCoupledADMM` evaluate a solver's effective masses again. The default covers body, body inertial, joint DOF, armature and reference-pose updates, so `SolverMuJoCo` entries pick up armature and reference-pose changes.
