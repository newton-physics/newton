Deprecate per-point `physics:masses` on `UsdGeom.Points` prims carrying `NewtonPointsDeformableSimAPI` in favor of `newton:masses`, matching the property rename in [newton-usd-schemas PR #105](https://github.com/newton-physics/newton-usd-schemas/pull/105). `ModelBuilder.add_usd()` now reads `newton:masses` first. An authored `physics:masses` is still used when `newton:masses` is unauthored and emits a `NewtonDeprecationWarning`; when both are authored, `newton:masses` wins and `physics:masses` is ignored with a warning. The fallback remains functional through 1.8 and is eligible for removal in 1.9. Rename the attribute to `newton:masses`. Typed `physics:masses` on AOUSD deformable simulation geometry is unaffected.
