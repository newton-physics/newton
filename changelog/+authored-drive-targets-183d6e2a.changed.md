Preserve explicit joint position targets outside joint limits, including USD drive targets used for preload, instead of replacing them with the limit midpoint. Omitted `JointDofConfig` targets retain their zero-or-midpoint default; revolute and prismatic builder methods preserve inherited `default_joint_cfg` targets. Reject nonfinite position targets. Callers that relied on automatic recentering, including inherited default targets outside new limits, must now supply the desired midpoint explicitly. This preserves stored targets only: `SolverXPBD` and `SolverVBD` still clamp drive targets to joint limits during solving, so this change does not enable out-of-range preload in those solvers.
