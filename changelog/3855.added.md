Add experimental implicit effort mode to `newton.actuators.Actuator` (`actuator.set_effort_mode_implicit(...)`, tuned via `newton.actuators.ActuatorImplicitOptions`, with `newton.actuators.ResponseOracle` providing articulation impulse responses): it solves actuator impulses jointly for each articulation before the solver step to support stable, stiff controllers. Use `ResponseOracle.refresh(state)` for a response assembled from the model, `ResponseOracle.refresh_from_inertia(solve_inverse, dof_map=...)` to reuse a solver's own joint-space inertia without materializing it, or `ResponseOracle.refresh_from_mass_matrix(mass_matrix, dof_map=...)` when the solver exposes a dense one.
