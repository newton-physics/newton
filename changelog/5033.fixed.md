Fix `SensorCamera` rendering of translucent Gaussian splats. Splats now blend front to back over whatever lies behind them, the nearest shape or the clear color, instead of disappearing when their accumulated opacity is 51% or less and turning fully opaque above it. The depth, normal, and shape-index outputs report the splats where their accumulated opacity exceeds one half, and the packed color alpha carries their coverage. When several `Gaussian` assets cross a ray, the asset with the nearest splat is the one shaded. When `SensorCamera.GaussianRenderMode.QUALITY` gathers the splats over several passes, it no longer blends a splat twice, skips a splat at the same distance as another, or drops the farther splats in front of a shape.
