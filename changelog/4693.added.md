Honor MJCF ``<option><flag filterparent="disable"/>`` and the USD PhysicsScene flag ``mjc:flag:filterparent``: the importers create the affected non-fixed joints with ``collision_filter_parent=False`` (an authored USD ``physics:collisionEnabled`` still wins), so parent and child bodies can collide as in native MuJoCo. The per-joint choice is exported as ``Model.joint_collision_filter_parent``.
