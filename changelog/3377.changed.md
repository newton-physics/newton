With `rigid_compliant_alm=True`, `SolverVBD` now enables torsional and rolling friction through the existing nonzero `ShapeConfig.mu_torsional` and `ShapeConfig.mu_rolling` defaults. Sliding and rolling use full 2D response metrics with matching disk projections; torsion remains scalar. Use a conservative contact solve metric for rigid bodies updated simultaneously in the same color. Avoid spurious angular motion from identical orientations when evaluating friction and angular velocity. Preserve the distinct normal and sliding force moments in proxy coupling when contact offsets rotate. Set both angular coefficients to zero on either shape in a contact pair to disable angular friction for that pair. Legacy contact paths remain sliding-only.
