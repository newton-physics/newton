Change `SolverVBD` particle self-contact Divide-and-Truncate to share the rigid-soft plane machinery: division planes are now built from certified primitive-pair separators (the closest-point axis is certified against the complete vertex-triangle or edge-edge pair, with a sweep of candidate axes as fallback), truncation uses endpoint signs so a vertex already across its plane may move back but not deeper, and a scale-adaptive float32 separation band replaces the fixed `[0.05, 0.95]` plane-placement clamp. Pairs that are exactly touching or intersecting at a collision-detection reference have no strict separator and are held at that reference until a later detection finds them apart; start simulations from a self-intersection-free configuration.
