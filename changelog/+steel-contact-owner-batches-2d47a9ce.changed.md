Speed up `SolverSteel` on large batches of worlds in contact: the serial articulation-local owners of contact-bearing worlds (the pair and residual-pair solves) are selected only while the world count is at most the device's SM count, contact-free worlds eligible for the single-articulation solve keep it at every count, and the general matrix-free sweep beside the local owners launches one block per world. The solver equations are unchanged; a world that moves from a local owner to the general sweep differs within the solver's iteration tolerance. No migration is required.
