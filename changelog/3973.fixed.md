Document `Model.tri_materials` in the units its kernels actually consume: `k_mu` and `k_lambda` in [N/m], `k_damp` in [N·s/m], and `k_drag` and `k_lift` in [kg/m³], rather than [Pa] and [Pa·s]. The triangle energy is integrated over area and a shell carries no thickness, so a modulus `E` enters as `E*h`. `Model.tet_materials` is integrated over volume and remains in [Pa]. The `ModelBuilder.default_tri_*` attributes now state these units too.
