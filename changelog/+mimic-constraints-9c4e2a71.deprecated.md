Deprecate the names that only served the removed mimic constraints: `SolverMuJoCo.mjc_eq_to_newton_mimic`, whose entries are now all `-1`, `Model.AttributeFrequency.CONSTRAINT_MIMIC`, and the `"constraint_mimic"` custom-attribute reference, which have no entries. Reading the mapping or declaring a custom attribute with the frequency or reference emits a `NewtonDeprecationWarning`. Remove their use, or read `SolverMuJoCo.mjc_eq_to_newton_joint_mimic` for the equality constraints of mimic joints.
