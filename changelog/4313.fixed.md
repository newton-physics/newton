Fix `SolverXPBD` hinges (revolute joints and D6 joints with one rotational axis) whose limit range extends beyond ±π, or that overshoot a limit near π: the angle is now measured within π of the middle of the limit range (or of the position target for unlimited joints) instead of as a principal value, so crossing ±π no longer reads as a ~2π limit violation. `newton.eval_ik` reports limited hinge angles on the same branch.
