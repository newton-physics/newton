Deprecate `ModelBuilder.add_rod(positions=...)`, `ModelBuilder.add_rod_graph()`, `newton.utils.CableStiffness`, and the four released `create_*` cable helpers in favor of `newton.Rod` and `ModelBuilder.add_rod(rod=...)`. The old forms remain functional through 1.6 and are eligible for removal in 1.7; the deprecated stiffness helper preserves its released conditional return shape.
