Deprecate `newton.utils.CableStiffness` and the four released `create_*` cable helpers in favor of `newton.rod.RodStiffness`, `newton.rod.stiffness_from_elastic_moduli()`, `newton.rod.compute_parallel_transport_quaternions()`, `newton.rod.generate_straight_points()`, and `newton.rod.generate_straight_points_and_quaternions()`. The old names remain functional through 1.6 and are eligible for removal in 1.7. The deprecated stiffness helper retains its released conditional return shape: it yields an unnamed `(stretch, bend)` pair when neither torsional-material input is supplied and a three-field `RodStiffness` when exactly one is supplied. The replacement instead requires exactly one of `poissons_ratio` and `shear_modulus` and always returns `RodStiffness`.
