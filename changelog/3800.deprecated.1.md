Deprecate `newton.utils.CableStiffness` and the four released `create_*` cable helpers in favor of `newton.utils.RodStiffness`, `newton.utils.rod_compute_stiffness_from_elastic_moduli()`, `newton.utils.rod_compute_parallel_transport_quaternions()`, `newton.utils.rod_generate_straight_points()`, and `newton.utils.rod_generate_straight_points_and_quaternions()`. The old names remain functional through 1.6 and are eligible for removal in 1.7. The deprecated stiffness helper preserves its released return contract: `(stretch, bend)` when neither `poissons_ratio` nor `shear_modulus` is supplied, and `RodStiffness` when exactly one is supplied. The replacement requires exactly one and always returns `RodStiffness`.
