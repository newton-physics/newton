Enforce the `min_distance` and `max_distance` of DISTANCE joints that root their articulation in `SolverSteel` when `enable_joint_limits=True`, as unilateral joint-limit rows on the distance between the joint's anchors (a `min_distance` of 0 is no bound, as in `SolverXPBD`, and a `max_distance` of 0 bounds the anchor offset on each axis; with the child's center of mass offset from its anchor, the anchor can pass a positive `max_distance` or drift from a zero one by an amount that a shorter timestep reduces); other DISTANCE joints in an articulation tree raise `NotImplementedError` when joint limits are enabled.
