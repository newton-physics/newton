Remove the `request_state_attributes` argument of `SensorIMU` and the `request_contact_attributes` argument of `SensorContact`, deprecated in Newton 1.7, even when passed as `False`. Sensors no longer request `State.body_qdd` or `Contacts.force` and read only solver observables. `SensorIMU.update()` and `SensorContact.update()` now require `observables=`: allocate them with `solver.observables(kinds=sensor.solver_observable_kinds)` (adding `contacts=contacts` for `SensorContact`), pass them to `solver.step()`, then to `update()`.
