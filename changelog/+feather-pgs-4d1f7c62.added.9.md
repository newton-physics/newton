Add to `SolverFeatherPGS`: shared contact anchors (`contact_shared_anchor`, `contact_friction_shared_anchor`), which apply contact or friction rows at the witness-point midpoint as PhysX does; row high-water marks (`row_watermark`, read with `constraint_row_watermarks()`); the experimental matrix-free row schedules `pgs_schedule="contact_then_internal"` and `"physx_grasp"`; and the default-off `use_parallel_streams` and `double_buffer` options, which run the size groups' factorization and response kernels on streams of their own and clear the mass-matrix and Jacobian buffers of the previous step on a separate stream (call `seed_double_buffer_events()` inside a CUDA graph capture before the first double-buffered step). With `use_parallel_streams`, groups of articulations with one shared joint topology assemble and factor their mass matrices in one kernel. None of these change the default solve.
