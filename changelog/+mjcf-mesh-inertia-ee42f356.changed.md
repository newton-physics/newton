Compute the mass properties of MJCF mesh geoms without an `inertia` attribute with MuJoCo's default `legacy` mode, which sums absolute tetrahedron volumes. Non-convex, open, or disconnected meshes now get MuJoCo's mass instead of the exact signed volume, and a mesh with too little volume for its mode, which MuJoCo rejects, adds no mass and logs a warning. Set `inertia="exact"` on the `<mesh>` asset to keep the previous mass properties, or `inertia="shell"` to give flat or open surface meshes mass.
