Fix `SolverFeatherPGS` dropping contacts between a global (world -1) kinematic body and the bodies of worlds other than 0 without reporting them. With `pgs_mode="matrix_free"` and `articulated_contact_response="immediate"`, a kinematic global free body now supports the bodies of every world. Contacts that still cannot be solved per world (a dynamic global body, a kinematic global articulation with joints, or a kinematic global free body in other modes, touching another world's articulated bodies) are dropped explicitly and flag both worlds in `constraint_overflow`, and the constructor warns when the model allows them.
