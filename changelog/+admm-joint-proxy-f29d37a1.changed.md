Realize the local ADMM joint penalty of `SolverCoupledADMM.Config.joint_proximal_bodies` with joint proxies: each entry view keeps the body across a cross-solver joint, placed with the joint at zero coordinates on the owned body and carrying the joint rows' `rho * W**2` inertia, centered on the rows. Proxies follow the owned body's ADMM iterate, so they change convergence only, not the converged answer, and keep high `rho` values stable. Proxy joints carry no drives, limits, friction, `joint_f`, or `joint_act`, and a cross joint outside any articulation joins the owned body's articulation in that view. A body joined to an entry by several cross-solver loop joints gets a separate proxy row in that view for each joint, so closed loops keep every joint row on a proxy. Proxy bodies carry `BodyFlags.PROXY` and every flag of their shapes is cleared.
