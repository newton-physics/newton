Realize the local joint penalty of `SolverCoupledADMM.Config.joint_proximal_bodies` with joint proxies: each entry view keeps the body across a cross-solver joint, flagged `BodyFlags.PROXY` and carrying the joint rows' `rho * W**2` inertia, so the penalty changes convergence, not the converged answer, and high `rho` values stay stable. Views now contain these extra body and joint rows; set `joint_proximal_bodies=False` to keep views without them, or list the entries that keep proxies in `joint_proximal_destination_entries`.
