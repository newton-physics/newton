Fix MJCF `<position>` and `<velocity>` joint actuators with a scalar `gear` on a hinge or slide joint being driven with unit gear by `SolverMuJoCo`. The importer now folds the gear into the joint-target drive (`kp`, `kv`, `damping` and `armature` times `gear**2`, `forcerange` times `gear`, `ctrlrange` divided by `gear`), so `Control.joint_target_q` and `joint_target_qd` stay in joint units; a gear on a multi-DOF joint, a vector gear or a zero gear still imports as `SolverMuJoCo.CtrlSource.CTRL_DIRECT`.
