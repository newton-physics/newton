Add the `reuse_kinematics` option of `newton.solvers.SolverSteel` (default on): the kinematics and inverse-dynamics terms formed when a step publishes its body state are carried into the next step, which skips re-deriving them from the joint coordinates. `reset` with a world mask, `notify_model_changed` and a coupler's input-state notice drop the carried terms; a caller that overwrites the next input state's joint coordinates in place calls the new `notify_state_changed`. The default publishes the body velocities through the solver's own kinematics instead of `eval_fk`, which changes them in their last bit; `reuse_kinematics=False` restores the previous publication bitwise.
