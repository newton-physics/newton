Add the opt-in `reuse_kinematics` option of `newton.solvers.SolverSteel` (off by default, so nothing changes unless a caller turns it on): the kinematics and inverse-dynamics terms formed when a step publishes its body state are carried into the next step, which skips re-deriving them from the joint coordinates. `reset` with a world mask, `notify_model_changed` and a coupler's input-state notice drop the carried terms; a caller that overwrites or replaces `joint_q`, `joint_qd` or `body_q` of the next input state outside `step` calls the new `notify_state_changed(state, world_mask=...)` first. The carry runs on CUDA only and stays off with gradients, joint velocity limits, sleeping, the parallel tree, or a FREE or DISTANCE joint that is not the first joint of its articulation; the `reuse_kinematics` attribute reports whether it runs. With the carry on, the published body poses and velocities come from the solver's own kinematics and can differ from `eval_fk`'s in their last bit. No migration is required.
