Simulate the springs, triangles, and tetrahedra of a model in `SolverMuJoCo` instead of ignoring them; such models require `use_mujoco_contacts=True`. Elements whose particles all lack `ParticleFlags.ACTIVE` are skipped, and particles that belong to no element, such as MPM grains, are still left to other solvers. To simulate deformables with another solver alongside MuJoCo, use `SolverCoupled` and assign their particles to the other solver's entry. When flexes are created and no `integrator` is passed, an `"implicit"` or `"implicitfast"` integrator, including the default, becomes `"discrete"`, which MuJoCo requires to integrate flex elasticity; pass `integrator="implicitfast"` to keep the previous integrator, at the cost of leaving 2D and 3D flex elasticity unintegrated.
