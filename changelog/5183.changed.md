Simulate the springs, triangles, and tetrahedra of a model in `SolverMuJoCo` instead of ignoring them; such models require `use_mujoco_contacts=True`. To simulate deformables with another solver alongside MuJoCo, use `SolverCoupled` and assign their particles to the other solver's entry. Particles that belong to no element, such as MPM grains, are still left to other solvers.
