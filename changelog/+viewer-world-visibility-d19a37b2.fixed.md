Keep world positions fixed when toggling visibility in the ViewerGL explorer, including worlds hidden during layout, and filter hidden worlds from shape and SDF instance batches. Add `reflow=False` to `ViewerBase.set_visible_worlds()` for callers that want to preserve the existing world layout. ViewerGL also applies world offsets and visibility to loose particles and deformable surfaces, including in legacy and headless modes; other viewer backends retain their existing deformable placement.
