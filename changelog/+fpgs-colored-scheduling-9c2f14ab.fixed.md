Speed up `SolverFeatherPGS` `articulated_contact_response="propagation-colored"` on dense single-world scenes: contact units are colored by first-fit greedy edge coloring over units sorted by contact index (deterministic for a given contact set) instead of round-based ticket bidding, kinematic free rigid bodies no longer count as coloring conflicts, and the coloring keeps its state in global scratch so the former 4096-unit shared-memory cap on the row budget is gone; the color budget rises from 256 to 512 so dense mesh piles no longer spill a quarter of their units into the serial tail. A 235-body pile went from 255 colors with half the units in the serial overflow tail to 67 colors with none, and the sweep from 7.9 ms to 1.34 ms per iteration.
