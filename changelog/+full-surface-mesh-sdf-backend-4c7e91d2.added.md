Add `CollisionPipeline(rigid_soft_full_surface_mesh_backend="sdf")` to generate full-surface deformable-rigid contacts for rigid meshes from their volume SDF instead of BVH feature pairs. Depth and direction then remain defined deep inside a mesh, which is more robust for solvers that do not keep deformables penetration-free. Meshes without a volume SDF keep BVH feature contacts, with a warning.
