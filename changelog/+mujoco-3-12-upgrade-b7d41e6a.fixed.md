Detect immovable bodies in the Newton-to-MuJoCo contact converter from the degrees of freedom of a body's weld group instead of testing `body_weldid == 0`. MuJoCo 3.12 gives mocap bodies their own weld id, so contacts between two immovable bodies were no longer filtered when either side was a Newton kinematic body or an articulated fixed root, both of which map to MuJoCo mocap bodies.
