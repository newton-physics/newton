Apply `steel:rigid_body_max_depenetration_velocity` to penetrating non-compliant contacts of articulation links under `SolverSteel`'s default `articulated_contact_response="immediate"`, matching `"propagation"` and PhysX. On every contact route, a contact now takes the smaller positive bound of its two bodies, including kinematic and zero-DOF bodies, even when one of them has a zero bound.
