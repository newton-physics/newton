Add per-actuator arrays: `Model.AttributeFrequency.ACTUATOR` and `BATTERY` with `Model.actuator_count` and `Model.battery_count`, `ModelBuilder.add_actuator()` returns the index of the actuator, the model stores the DOF of each actuator as `actuation.actuator_dof`, and actuators built by `ModelBuilder` write their effort before clamping to `control.actuator_computed_effort` and the effort they apply to the simulation to `control.actuator_applied_effort`. Drives read per-actuator inputs by declaring them in `DriveBase.custom_inputs` with the frequency `Model.AttributeFrequency.ACTUATOR`, and `Actuator` takes `actuator_indices`, with which `control_computed_output_attr` and the new `control_applied_output_attr` are written per actuator. `Actuator.get_attribute_frequency()`, `Control.get_attribute_frequency()` and `get_attribute_frequency()` on the containers from `Actuator.sim_state()` and `Actuator.sim_control()` report the frequency of actuator parameters and input arrays.
