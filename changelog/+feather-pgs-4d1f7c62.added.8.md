Add the experimental `articulated_contact_response="propagation"`, `"propagation-fused"` and `"propagation-colored"` options to `newton.solvers.SolverFeatherPGS` (matrix-free solve only), which solve contacts of articulated bodies as body-space rows with responses from an articulated-body factorization of each tree, `"propagation-colored"` in parallel batches of contacts that share no body; `propagation_same_articulation_rows` for contacts between links of the same articulation; and `propagation_cached_response` with `propagation_cached_response_max_bodies`, which apply cached per-body responses instead of the per-iteration tree walk. The propagation rows support restitution, contact regularization, warm start, velocity-only iterations, friction patches, PGS drive rows and mimic rows; the split solve, contact torsion, contact compliance, sleeping and loop-closing joints raise `NotImplementedError`.
