Add experimental, opt-in passive-island sleeping to `SolverFeatherPGS` (`enable_sleeping=False` by default). Articulations in contact are joined into islands every step; a supported island that stays below `sleep_linear_threshold` and `sleep_angular_threshold` for `sleep_quiet_time` freezes its published state and, with `sleep_skip_constraints=True`, skips its contact and joint-limit rows, its friction-patch rebuild and its articulated dynamics. External or nonfinite forces, state and gravity changes, contacts with awake or moving bodies, lost support or row capacity, `reset()` of its world and `notify_model_changed()` for its own properties wake it. Sleeping works under CUDA graph capture; it is rejected with `pgs_warmstart`, `pgs_velocity_iterations` and `pgs_mode="split"`.
