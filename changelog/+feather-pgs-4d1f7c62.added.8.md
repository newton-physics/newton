Add the experimental `articulated_contact_response="propagation"` and `"propagation-fused"` options to `newton.solvers.SolverFeatherPGS` (matrix-free solve only), which solve contacts of articulated bodies as body-space rows with responses from an articulated-body factorization of each tree, and `propagation_same_articulation_rows` for contacts between links of the same articulation. The split solve and the combinations that the propagation rows do not implement raise `NotImplementedError`.
