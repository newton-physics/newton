Fix `SolverCoupled` entries that own a body whose incoming articulation joint from a parent body the entry view hides: reduced-coordinate solvers welded the body in place and `SolverMuJoCo` raised. The compact entry view now gives such a body a FREE root joint matching `ModelBuilder.add_joint_free()`, including the registered defaults of custom attributes. An entry that owns a body but not its non-FREE joint to the world now logs a warning, since no entry simulates that joint.
