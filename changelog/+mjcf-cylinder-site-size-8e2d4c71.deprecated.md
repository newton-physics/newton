Deprecate the `mujoco:site_size_is_display` shape attribute registered by `SolverMuJoCo`. The MJCF importer no longer populates it. Passing it to `ModelBuilder.add_site()` emits a deprecation warning and sets `scale[2]` of a cylinder site to `0.0`. Set `scale[2]` of a cylinder site to `0.0` directly instead.
