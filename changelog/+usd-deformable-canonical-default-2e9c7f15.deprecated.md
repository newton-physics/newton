Deprecate reading legacy vendor-namespaced deformable material attributes (`omniphysics:`, `physxDeformableBody:`) from a material that applies `PhysicsVolumeDeformableMaterialAPI` when `compat_namespaces` is omitted in `newton.usd.get_tetmesh()` and `newton.TetMesh.create_from_usd()`. The earlier default migration did not warn about this case, so attributes without a `physics:` counterpart are still read, with a `NewtonDeprecationWarning` naming them; a future release will ignore them. Author the canonical `physics:` attributes, or pass `compat_namespaces=newton.usd.DEFORMABLE_LEGACY_NAMESPACES` to keep reading them.
