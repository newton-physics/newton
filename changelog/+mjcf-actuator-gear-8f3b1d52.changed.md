Import MJCF `<position>` and `<velocity>` joint actuators whose `gear` differs from `1 0 0 0 0 0` as `SolverMuJoCo.CtrlSource.CTRL_DIRECT`, matching the USD importer. `SolverMuJoCo` rebuilt them as joint-target drives with unit gear, so their stiffness and damping were `gear**2` times too small and their force range was not scaled. Drive them through `Control.mujoco.ctrl` in actuator units (`ctrl = gear * target`); their `joint_target_ke`, `joint_target_kd` and `joint_target_mode` are no longer set. To keep joint targets, remove the `gear` and scale `kp`/`kv` by `gear**2` and `forcerange` by `|gear|`.
