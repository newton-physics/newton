Import MJCF `<position>` and `<velocity>` joint actuators whose `gear` differs from `1 0 0 0 0 0` as `SolverMuJoCo.CtrlSource.CTRL_DIRECT`, matching the USD importer. `SolverMuJoCo` rebuilt them as joint-target drives with unit gear, so their stiffness and damping were `gear**2` times too small and their force range was not scaled. Drive them through `Control.mujoco.ctrl` in actuator units: `ctrl = gear * (target_q + ref)` for a position actuator, where `ref` is the joint's MuJoCo `ref` in meters or radians (usually zero), and `ctrl = gear * target_qd` for a velocity actuator (no reference offset); their `joint_target_ke`, `joint_target_kd` and `joint_target_mode` are no longer set. An authored `ctrlrange` limits `ctrl` in actuator units, so it must cover `gear * (target_q + ref)` for a position actuator and `gear * target_qd` for a velocity actuator. To keep joint targets instead (scalar one-DOF joints only), remove the `gear` from the actuator and convert the rest: scale `kp` and `kv` by `gear**2`, divide `ctrlrange` by `gear` and multiply `forcerange` by `gear`, swapping the lower and upper bounds when `gear` is negative (for example `gear="-2"` with `forcerange="0 10"` becomes `forcerange="-20 0"`, and `gear="3"` with `ctrlrange="-3 3"` becomes `ctrlrange="-1 1"`).
