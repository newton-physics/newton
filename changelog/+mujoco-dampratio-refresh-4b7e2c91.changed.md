Resolve `dampratio` on `SolverMuJoCo` direct position actuators whenever model changes refresh MuJoCo's constants, from each world's physical inertia. Damping now follows inertia edits notified without `ModelFlags.ACTUATOR_PROPERTIES`, and actuators below a kinematic root no longer use the root's locked inertia. Only actuators that hold a `dampratio` at solver construction are resolved; to add damping to another actuator at runtime, set its `biasprm[2]` to the negative damping.
