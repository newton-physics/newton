Add an experimental BVH back-end for full-surface rigid-soft contact against triangle/convex mesh shapes, opt-in via `CollisionPipeline(full_surface_mesh_backend="bvh")`: three discrete feature queries (soft vertex vs rigid triangle, soft triangle vs rigid vertex, soft edge vs rigid edge) resolve exact mesh geometry with no SDF provisioning required. New `CollisionPipeline.refit_soft_contact_bvh` / `rebuild_soft_contact_bvh` keep its soft-contact BVHs fresh, and `verify_buffers` now also reports soft-contact buffer overflow. The default `"sdf"` back-end is unchanged.
