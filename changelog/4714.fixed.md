Fix `SolverImplicitMPM` producing a NaN state when a viscous material shares its grid with inactive particles. A grid node whose particles are all inactive has no material on it, so its volume and its volume-averaged viscosity are both zero. Dividing one by the other to build the viscous regularizer is `0/0`, and the resulting NaN propagates to the whole state instead of staying on the empty nodes. The regularizer is now skipped on nodes with no material.
