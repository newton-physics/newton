Add `Model.body_gravity_scale` to scale the gravity force on each rigid body. Set it with the `gravity_scale` argument of `ModelBuilder.add_body()` and `ModelBuilder.add_link()`, or import it from USD `newton:gravityScale` and, with `SchemaResolverPhysx`, `physxRigidBody:disableGravity`. `SolverFeatherstone`, `SolverSteel`, `SolverMuJoCo`, `eval_inverse_dynamics_passive()` and the coupling gravity hook apply it; `SolverSemiImplicit`, `SolverXPBD`, `SolverVBD` and `SolverKamino` raise `NotImplementedError` when a dynamic body has a scale other than 1.
