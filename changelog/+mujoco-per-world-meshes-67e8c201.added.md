Support different mesh assets and convex-hull counts across compatible worlds in `SolverMuJoCo`. Differing hull counts with native MuJoCo Warp contacts require a `mujoco_warp` build containing google-deepmind/mujoco_warp#1689; Newton-generated contacts work with the 3.14.0 release. With native MuJoCo contacts, corresponding shapes must now also agree on `has_shape_collision`; previously the first world's flag silently applied to all worlds.
