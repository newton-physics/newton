Fix MJCF vector attributes given with fewer components than their length, such as `gainprm="0.75 1.05 300"`, `solref="0.02"` or a one-value box `size`. They now keep the remaining components from the default class, as MuJoCo does, instead of zero-padding or replicating them; partial muscle parameters no longer fail `SolverMuJoCo` conversion with `lmin<1<lmax required in muscle`.
