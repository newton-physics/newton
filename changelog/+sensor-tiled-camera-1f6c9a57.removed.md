Remove `newton.sensors.SensorTiledCamera`, deprecated in Newton 1.7; use `newton.sensors.SensorCamera` instead. It takes one `camera_transforms` entry per view instead of a `(camera_count, world_count)` grid, shared `(height, width, sample_count, 2)` rays from `SensorCamera.compute_camera_rays_pinhole()` or a fisheye helper, a `world_indices` entry mapping each view to its world, and output images of shape `(view_count, height, width)` allocated with `SensorCamera.allocate_image_*()`; the image helpers move from `sensor.utils` to `SensorCamera.Utils`.
