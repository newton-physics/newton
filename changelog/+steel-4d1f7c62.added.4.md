Add persistent friction patches, contact regularization, restitution from `ShapeConfig.restitution` (zero by default), contact-identity warm start, velocity-only iterations and gap gates for speculative contacts to `SolverSteel` (`friction_anchor_beta`, `contact_regularization`, `restitution_velocity_threshold`, `contact_warmstart`, `velocity_iterations`, `contact_gap_gate`, `contact_friction_gap_max`; `None` turns either gate off, so `contact_gap_gate=0` keeps touching contacts only). The default `friction_anchor_beta=None` selects friction patches, which hold static loads without creep, in the matrix-free solve and point friction in the split solve; pass `friction_anchor_beta=0` for point friction.
