Add experimental implicit compliance of hydroelastic contacts to `SolverSteel` (`contact_compliance`): contacts with positive stiffness are solved as unilateral spring-dampers with the contact damping and friction weight. It requires point friction, which an omitted `friction_anchor_beta` selects (with a warning) when compliance is enabled. Compliant rows are prepared on the device, so steps can be captured in a CUDA graph; `check_constraint_capacity()` reports invalid input from captured steps.
