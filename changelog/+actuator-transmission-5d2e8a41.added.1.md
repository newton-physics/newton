Add actuator sensors, which measure the joint positions and velocities before the transmission maps them: `newton.actuators.SensorBase` and `SensorEncoder`, an encoder behind a passive output joint, e.g. a backlash joint. With a gear, the drive reads `gear · (q_actuated + q_backlash)` and `gear · (q̇_actuated + q̇_backlash)`, in actuator units; the clamps and the motor read the velocity of the simulated joints, `gear · q̇_actuated`. Pass them through `Actuator(sensor=...)`, read them from `Actuator.sensor`, or use `ModelBuilder.add_actuator(sensor=(SensorEncoder, {"output_joint": ...}))`, which stores them in the new `ModelBuilder.ActuatorEntry.sensor_class`, `sensor_shared_kwargs` and `sensor_args` and groups actuators by sensor. USD authors the encoder with `NewtonActuatorEncoderAPI`, which maps to `ComponentKind.SENSOR`.
