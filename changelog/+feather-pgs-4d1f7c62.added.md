Add the experimental `newton.solvers.SolverFeatherPGS`, which combines Featherstone articulated-body dynamics with a projected Gauss-Seidel solve of contacts with coupled Coulomb friction, optional joint limits (`enable_joint_limits`, off by default) and joint velocity limits, and integrates joint drives implicitly. Passive joint springs are not applied yet: a model with nonzero `mujoco:dof_passive_stiffness` warns at construction. The default `pgs_mode="matrix_free"` is a fused CUDA solve that supports every option; `pgs_mode="split"` assembles a dense Delassus matrix per world, runs on CPU and CUDA (pass it explicitly on CPU) and raises when an option that only the matrix-free solve implements is active (`NotImplementedError`, except positive contact torsion on CPU, which raises `ValueError`). Rows beyond the per-world capacities are dropped and reported in `constraint_overflow`, with the `(world_count + 1,)` layout of the reset mask; the solver supports world-masked `reset()` and CUDA graph capture. Contacts that couple a dynamic global body, or a kinematic global articulation with joints, to another world's bodies cannot be solved; they are dropped and flagged, and the constructor warns when the model allows them. Unsupported model features, including enabled MuJoCo equality constraints that were not converted to Newton loop joints or mimic constraints, raise `NotImplementedError` instead of being ignored.
