Add experimental, solver-owned `SolverBase.Observables` containers for rigid-body accelerations, parent-joint wrenches, contact forces, and MuJoCo actuator forces (`SolverMuJoCo.ObservableKind.QFRC_ACTUATOR`). Allocate reusable arrays with `solver.observables(*, kinds=None, contacts=None, requires_grad=None)`: omitted or `None` kinds allocate all observables supported by the configured solver; `kinds=` selects a collection of names from `solver.ObservableKind` or literal strings, and an empty collection allocates no arrays. Populate them with `solver.step(..., observables=observables)` and use `observables.select(kinds)` for subsets that share arrays and gradients, including for substep scheduling and graph capture.

Compose sensor requirements through `sensor.solver_observable_kinds` and pass the container to `SensorIMU.update()`, `SensorContact.update()`, and `ViewerBase.log_contacts()` using `observables=`. Construct a `CollisionPipeline` and pass its contacts as `contacts=` when requesting contact-indexed observables, including zero-capacity fields. Allocation validates the contact device and capacities and binds that storage; steps and consumers require the same instance. Contact sensors and viewers can read the initially zeroed arrays before the first step.
