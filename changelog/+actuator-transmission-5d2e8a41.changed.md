Change actuator index arrays to name one or more joints per actuator: `Actuator` accepts 2-D `indices`, `pos_indices` and `effort_indices` with shape `(joints, N)`, and `ModelBuilder.add_actuator()` accepts a sequence for `index` and `pos_index`; several joints require a transmission other than `TransmissionDirect`. Actuators that drive one joint keep 1-D index arrays of shape `(N,)` and one int per actuator in `ModelBuilder.ActuatorEntry.indices`, `pos_indices` and `effort_indices`; actuators that drive several joints have arrays of shape `(joints, N)` and one tuple of DOFs per actuator. `ArticulationView.get_actuator_parameter()` and `set_actuator_parameter()` map each actuator to its first DOF only.
