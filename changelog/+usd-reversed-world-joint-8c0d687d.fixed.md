Fix `ModelBuilder.add_usd()` mirroring the motion of joints whose `physics:body1` is the world or a static prim. This covers revolute and prismatic joints, and D6 joints or single-axis joints merged into one D6 joint that have no rotational axis, or one rotational axis with every translational axis parallel to it. Their drives, limits, and initial joint state now act in the authored direction. The importer negates the joint axes in `Model.joint_axis` for these joints and keeps the authored values, so `joint_q` equals the authored joint position. Remove any sign workarounds for these joints only; other swapped D6 and merged joints keep their previous behavior (see the deprecation entry).
