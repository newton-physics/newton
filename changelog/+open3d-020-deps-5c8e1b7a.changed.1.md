Require `open3d` 0.20 or newer in the `importers` and `remesh` extras. `open3d` is now installed on Python 3.13 and newer and on Linux aarch64, but no longer on Intel macOS, where Poisson remeshing and `Gaussian.create_from_ply()` are unavailable. Upgrade `open3d` if `Gaussian.create_from_ply()` raises an `ImportError` about the `open3d` version.
