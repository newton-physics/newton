Add `SolverMuJoCo` hooks for coupling learned dynamics models with MuJoCo Warp:

  - Experimental `SolverMuJoCo.set_qacc_smooth_override()` replaces MuJoCo
    Warp's pre-constraint joint accelerations on selected DOFs before the
    constraint solve. It is CUDA-graph-capturable and leaves the step unchanged
    when unset.
  - Experimental `SolverMuJoCo.ObservableKind.QACC_SMOOTH` exports the
    pre-constraint joint accelerations in Newton DOF order.
  - Read-only `SolverMuJoCo.use_speculative_contacts` property.
