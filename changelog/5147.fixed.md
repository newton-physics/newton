Add `parse_sensors` to `ModelBuilder.add_mjcf` to import MJCF `<sensor>` elements into the `mujoco:sensor` custom frequency, which `SolverMuJoCo` exports as native MuJoCo sensors. Imports without `parse_sensors=True` still ignore the `<sensor>` section. With it, unsupported or unresolved sensors are logged once per file, except sensors on sites skipped by `parse_sites=False`.
