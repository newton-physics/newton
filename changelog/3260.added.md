Add experimental `ParticleFlags.KINEMATIC` and `Control.particle_target_q` to drive deformable particles to prescribed positions in `SolverVBD`, `SolverXPBD`, `SolverSemiImplicit`, `SolverFeatherstone`, and `SolverCoupled`. Coupled entries with kinematic particles require `substeps=1`, and kinematic particles cannot be coupling proxies.
