Add torsional and rolling friction to `SolverVBD` rigid-rigid contacts with `rigid_compliant_alm=True`, including torque transfer through VBD proxy coupling.
The length-valued coefficients `ShapeConfig.mu_torsional` and `ShapeConfig.mu_rolling` [m] must be finite and nonnegative.
Their nonzero defaults enable angular friction; set both coefficients to zero on either shape to disable it for that pair.
