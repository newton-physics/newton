Add torsional and rolling friction to `SolverVBD` compliant-ALM rigid-rigid contacts, including torque transfer through VBD proxy coupling.
Use the existing `ShapeConfig.mu_torsional` and `ShapeConfig.mu_rolling` coefficients [m], which VBD validates as finite and nonnegative.
Their nonzero defaults enable angular friction; set both coefficients to zero on either shape to disable it for that pair.
