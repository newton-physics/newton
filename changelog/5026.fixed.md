Fix `SolverSteel` letting friction coefficients that overflow float32 reach its contact rows. Shape pairs now average their coefficients without overflowing, a coefficient that is not finite after `contact_friction_scale` raises a `ValueError` at construction or on an eager notification and is reported by `check_constraint_capacity()` until the coefficients are finite again and the worlds of that shape are reset, and compliant contacts reject a friction weight whose product with the pair coefficient overflows.
