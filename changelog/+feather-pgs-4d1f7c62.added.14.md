Add `SolverFeatherPGS.register_custom_attributes()`, which registers the PhysX rigid-body USD attributes `physxRigidBody:angularDamping`, `maxLinearVelocity`, `maxAngularVelocity` and `maxDepenetrationVelocity` as the `physx:angular_damping`, `physx:max_linear_velocity`, `physx:max_angular_velocity` and `physx:max_depenetration_velocity` body attributes (for example `model.physx.angular_damping`), and `physxCollision:minTorsionalPatchRadius` as the `physx:min_torsional_patch_radius` shape attribute that enables contact torsion; the solver reads them every step.
