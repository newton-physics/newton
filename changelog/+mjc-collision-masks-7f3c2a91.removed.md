Remove reading MuJoCo collision masks from the `mjc:contype` and `mjc:conaffinity` USD attributes, which the MjcPhysics schema does not define. `ModelBuilder.add_usd()` now warns when a prim authors them and leaves the masks unset. Masks read from these attributes were stored but never affected collision filtering. USD can express collision filtering with `newton:selfCollisionEnabled`, `PhysicsFilteredPairsAPI`, or `PhysicsCollisionGroup`, although none of them is a drop-in replacement for MuJoCo's bitmasks.
