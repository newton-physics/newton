Speed up the `gravity_force` and `coriolis_force` outputs of `newton.eval_inverse_dynamics_passive()` on CUDA by evaluating RNEA with one thread per joint instead of one thread per articulation, for example about 14x for 1024 G1 robots. CPU devices and models without a tree level schedule (non-tree articulations or a tree level wider than 512 joints) keep the per-articulation path. Results can change within floating-point tolerance; no migration is required.
