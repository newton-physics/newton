Add `newton.ShapeFrictionMode` and `ModelBuilder.ShapeConfig.friction_mode` (`Model.shape_material_friction_mode`) to select which friction components a shape's contacts apply: frictionless, sliding, torsional, or rolling. A contact uses the higher mode of its two shapes, and particle contacts follow the shape's mode, so `NONE` makes them frictionless (`SolverMuJoCo` flexes keep sliding friction). `NONE` is supported by all built-in solvers; torsional and rolling modes by `SolverXPBD`, `SolverMuJoCo`, `SolverVBD`, and the LOX backend of `SolverKamino`, while other solvers apply sliding friction only and warn if they solve rigid contacts. The i-th colliders of a body's variants must share a friction mode. MJCF and URDF `condim`, USD `mjc:condim`, and torsional or rolling coefficients authored through `NewtonMaterialAPI` on non-MuJoCo colliders set the mode on import; a positive `physxCollision:minTorsionalPatchRadius` `r` selects torsional friction with `mu_torsional = mu * r`.
