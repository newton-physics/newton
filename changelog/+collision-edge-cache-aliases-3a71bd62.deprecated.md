Deprecate `Model.mesh_edge_indices`, `Model.mesh_edge_centers`, `Model.mesh_edge_halves`, and `Model.shape_edge_range` in Newton 1.8. These fields hold internal packed collision-edge acceleration data; their public aliases remain readable and writable with a `NewtonDeprecationWarning` until removal in 1.9. Use `CollisionPipeline` for model collision detection. Low-level `NarrowPhase` mesh-SDF collision relies on internal edge data without a stable public accessor.
