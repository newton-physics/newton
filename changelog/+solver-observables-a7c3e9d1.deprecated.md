Deprecate built-in solver-produced extended attributes on `State` and `Contacts`, their `Model` and `ModelBuilder` request methods, and force export through `update_contacts()`. Allocate with `solver.observables(kinds=kinds)`, pass the container to `solver.step(..., observables=observables)`, and read `observables.body_qdd`, `observables.body_parent_f`, `observables.contact_f`, or MuJoCo's `observables.qfrc_actuator` instead of the corresponding state/contact fields. MuJoCo and Kamino retain geometry-only `update_contacts()` without a warning when no `Contacts.force` array is allocated.

Deprecate `SensorIMU(request_state_attributes=True)` and `SensorContact(request_contact_attributes=True)`; each emits one caller-level warning. Both retain their `True` defaults during the deprecation period. Explicitly pass `False` and supply the observable container through `sensor.update(..., observables=observables)`.
