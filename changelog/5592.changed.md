Size `SolverSteel`'s `articulation_constraint_max` from the model when it is not given: the busiest world's joint rows plus three rows (four with contact torsion) per estimated contact that stays dense in that world (each contact counts toward the world of its articulation, world 0 for a global one, whatever the worlds of its shapes), between 32 and 256 rows unless the joint rows alone need more. With `articulated_contact_response="immediate"` these are the contacts of articulated bodies. With `"propagation"` only contacts between two links of the same articulation stay dense, and the estimate counts every contact between two articulated bodies as an upper bound (none when `propagation_same_articulation_rows` is set). Pass `articulation_constraint_max=32` to keep the previous fixed capacity.
