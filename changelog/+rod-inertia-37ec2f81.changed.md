Use solid-cylinder inertia, or open-tube inertia for hollow rods, for `ModelBuilder.add_rod()` segments and imported USD cables, in place of a capsule tensor scaled to the segment mass. Collision shapes stay capsules. At equal mass, a solid segment twice as long as its radius gains about 9% axial inertia and loses about 52% transverse inertia, so revalidate simulations and retune settings calibrated to the old inertia.
