Add experimental `SolverImplicitMPM.Config.carry_residual_strain` to keep the strain an unconverged solve leaves in the elastic strain (`"unconverged"`), or the full kinematic strain sampled at particles minus the plastic strain (`"sampled"`). The default `"none"` keeps the previous behavior and suits stiff materials; `"unconverged"` is suggested for compliant elastic materials, which otherwise relax at low iteration counts. The beam-twist, snow-ball, and pinch-carry examples use `"unconverged"`.
