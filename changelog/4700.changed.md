Use half the AOUSD default thickness (0.5 mm at `metersPerUnit=1`) as the fallback particle radius for USD volume deformables (`UsdGeom.TetMesh` with `PhysicsVolumeDeformableSimAPI`), matching the cloth and cable importers, instead of `ModelBuilder.default_particle_radius`. Setting `default_particle_radius` no longer affects these prims; to restore a larger radius, overwrite `ModelBuilder.particle_radius` over the soft body's particle range after `add_usd()` (the ranges are available in the `path_soft_map` result of `add_usd(..., return_deformable_results=True)`).
