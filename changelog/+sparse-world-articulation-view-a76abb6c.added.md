Support `ArticulationView` selections that match articulations in only some worlds, or whose rows are irregularly spaced between worlds: the view's world axis holds only the matching worlds (`ArticulationView.world_ids`, `ArticulationView.is_sparse`), and irregularly spaced rows are gathered and scattered through absolute model indices (`ArticulationView.uses_explicit_model_indices`). Shapes are addressed the same way when every selected articulation has the same shape count and owning links but other shapes are interleaved between its shapes at different positions.
