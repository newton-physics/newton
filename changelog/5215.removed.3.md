Remove `SolverVBD.set_joint_constraint_mode()` and the `vbd:joint_is_hard` custom attribute, deprecated in Newton 1.6, together with `SolverVBD.JointSlot`, whose only use was that method; compliant ALM has no hard/soft joint mode. Authoring `vbd:joint_is_hard` through `custom_attributes` now raises `AttributeError`, and USD-authored `newton:vbd:joint_is_hard` values are ignored. Delete these calls and attributes; to make non-Rod structural joints stiffer, raise `rigid_joint_linear_ke` and `rigid_joint_angular_ke`, and for Rod joints raise the Rod's stretch, shear, bend, or twist stiffness.
