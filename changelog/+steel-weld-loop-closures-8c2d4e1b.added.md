Add FIXED (weld) loop-closing joints to `SolverSteel` as three shared-anchor point rows plus three rotation rows. They support link-break loops, which split one body of a loop into a primary and a shadow welded back together so that every joint of the loop keeps its coordinate, drive and limits; see "Link-break loops" in the articulation docs. `enable_bilateral_preelimination` handles one weld per articulation within its 8-row capacity.
