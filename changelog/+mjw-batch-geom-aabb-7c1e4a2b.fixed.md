Recompute per-world `geom_aabb` and `geom_rbound` in `SolverMuJoCo` from each world's shape sizes for sphere, capsule, cylinder, ellipsoid and box shapes (and batch `geom_aabb` when `separate_worlds=True`), so MuJoCo Warp's broadphase no longer culls every world with world 0's bounds when those shapes differ in size or are resized at runtime. Plane bounds are rewritten with MuJoCo's size-independent values; mesh (including convex meshes and cones, which MuJoCo represents as meshes), SDF and height-field bounds are not recomputed and keep the values from `put_model`.
