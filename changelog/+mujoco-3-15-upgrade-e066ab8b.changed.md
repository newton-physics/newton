Require `mujoco~=3.15.0` and `mujoco-warp~=3.15.0` (previously `~=3.14.0`). Actuators that keep an unresolved `dampratio` (MJCF imported with `ctrl_direct=True`, USD `MjcActuator` prims, or `mujoco:actuator_biasprm` with a positive `biasprm[2]`) now derive damping from the operational-space inertia, which lowers damping on multi-link chains; set `kv` explicitly to pin a specific damping.
