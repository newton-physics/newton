Change experimental `JointType.ROD` from four VBD material slots to q7 relative-pose and qd6 linear/angular-twist state, with FK/IK support. Use `joint_q_start`, `joint_qd_start`, and `ModelBuilder.add_joint_rod()` instead of fixed Rod slices. Rod constitutive dynamics remain SolverVBD-only; SolverFeatherstone, inverse-dynamics evaluation, and the LM/LBFGS IK optimizers reject Rod.
