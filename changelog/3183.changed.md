Change experimental `JointType.ROD` from four VBD-specific material slots in `joint_q` and `joint_qd` to a seven-coordinate relative pose in `joint_q` and a six-DoF relative twist in `joint_qd`. `eval_fk()` and `eval_ik()` now convert between this state and body state. Use `joint_q_start` and `joint_qd_start` to locate each Rod joint's coordinate and velocity slices instead of assuming four entries, and construct Rod joints with `ModelBuilder.add_joint_rod()` to obtain the required canonical six-axis material layout. These axes use `JointTargetMode.NONE`, so material stiffness is no longer classified as actuation. Rod constitutive dynamics remain supported only by `SolverVBD`; `SolverFeatherstone`, inverse dynamics, `IKOptimizerLM`, and `IKOptimizerLBFGS` reject Rod joints.
