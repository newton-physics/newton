Deprecate runtime `Model.mujoco.dof_springref` in favor of coordinate-indexed `Model.joint_rest_q`. For models without joints that have more coordinates than DOFs (free, ball, distance, or rod) and without nonzero `mujoco:dof_ref`, the old name remains a warning alias to `joint_rest_q`; otherwise reads, assignments, and deletions raise an `AttributeError` with migration guidance. Convert absolute MuJoCo values with `rest_q = springref - ref`, index by `joint_q_start`, and notify the solver with `ModelFlags.JOINT_REFERENCE_POSE_PROPERTIES` or `ModelFlags.JOINT_DOF_PROPERTIES` after runtime edits.
