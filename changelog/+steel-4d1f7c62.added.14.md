Add `SolverSteel.register_custom_attributes()`, which registers the PhysX rigid-body USD attributes `physxRigidBody:angularDamping`, `maxLinearVelocity`, `maxAngularVelocity` and `maxDepenetrationVelocity` as the `steel:rigid_body_angular_damping`, `steel:rigid_body_max_linear_velocity`, `steel:rigid_body_max_angular_velocity` and `steel:rigid_body_max_depenetration_velocity` body attributes (for example `model.steel.rigid_body_angular_damping`), and `physxCollision:minTorsionalPatchRadius` as the `steel:min_torsional_patch_radius` shape attribute that enables contact torsion; the solver reads them every step.
