Interpret experimental `tendon_settle_tol` as a final capstan-force residual
tolerance instead of an update-size tolerance. Use `tendon_settle_atol` to
control the absolute force tolerance near zero tension; setting
`tendon_settle_tol=0` still disables early-out. The default maximum remains
256 sweeps, but `tendon_max_sweeps` now accepts larger positive 32-bit integers
for routes that need more iterations.
