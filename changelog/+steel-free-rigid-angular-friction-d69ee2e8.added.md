Add experimental spin and rolling friction rows to the free-body contacts of `newton.solvers.SolverSteel`, enabled by the contact's `newton.ShapeFrictionMode` (`TORSIONAL` adds a spin row bounded by `Model.shape_material_mu_torsional` times the normal load, `ROLLING` also a rolling pair bounded by `Model.shape_material_mu_rolling`). The matrix-free solve with the immediate articulated response is required; other configurations warn and keep sliding friction. `BODY_PARENT_F` counts their torques.
