Keep `SolverSteel`'s sparse mass factors with persistent friction patches on: the sparse contact rows place the tangent rows at the patch anchors and give normal-only rows to contacts whose anchor did not survive, and the sparse sweep pools the patch normal load over the region's linked normal rows, as the dense sweep does. Patches are the default friction, so default-configured branched articulations that meet the sparse conditions on CUDA now use the sparse factors, with or without free bodies, where the previous release used the dense factors; their results differ from it within float tolerance. No migration is required.
