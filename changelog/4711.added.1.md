Add per-shape and per-face collider materials to `SolverImplicitMPM` with:

  - `mpm:collider_adhesion` and `mpm:collider_projection_threshold` shape attributes.
  - `collider_material_counts` in `setup_collider()`, and `collider_face_materials()` and `collider_materials()` for writable per-face views.
