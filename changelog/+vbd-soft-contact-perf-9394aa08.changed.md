Accelerate `SolverVBD` rigid-soft contact processing and deformable elasticity on CUDA: contact forces are gathered per particle over the active contact prefix instead of scanning the full contact capacity every color and iteration (a win when the candidate set is much larger than the active contact count; at small capacities the previous scatter can be marginally faster), far-apart edge/face candidates are rejected with a conservative AABB test before the SDF search, the tiled elasticity solve packs two cloth particles per warp, and tetrahedra-only models use a specialized elasticity kernel. Solver settings, contact capacity, and the CPU and deterministic kernels are unchanged.
