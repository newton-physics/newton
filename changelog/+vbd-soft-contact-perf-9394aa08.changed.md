Accelerate `SolverVBD` rigid-soft contact processing and deformable elasticity on CUDA: gather contact forces per particle over the active contact prefix instead of scanning the full contact capacity every color and iteration, sort contact-detection candidate pairs by shape, reject far-apart edge/face candidates with a conservative AABB test before the SDF search, pack two cloth particles per warp in the tiled elasticity solve, and select a tetrahedra-only elasticity kernel when triangle and edge stiffnesses are inactive. Solver settings and contact capacity are unchanged, and CPU and deterministic modes keep the previous kernels. The shape-major candidate ordering applies on every device, so soft-contact records are emitted in a different order: the contact set is identical, but summation-order differences can shift results within floating-point tolerance on CPU and deterministic paths. Both contact-processing changes target scenes whose candidate sets are much larger than their active contacts (for example many replicated worlds): when most candidates are in active contact, the shape-major ordering can make the edge/face generation pass itself roughly 20% slower (full-step impact within 1-2%), and at small contact capacities (tens of thousands of slots) the per-particle gather kernel can be a few percent slower than the scatter it replaces, with no measurable full-step effect.
