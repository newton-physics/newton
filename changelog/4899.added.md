Add model cameras:

  - Declare cameras with `ModelBuilder.add_camera()` and `newton.CameraLensType`, stored in `Model.camera_*` arrays with a `CAMERA` attribute frequency; cameras replicate per world and follow `collapse_fixed_joints()`.
  - Import cameras from USD `UsdGeom.Camera` prims (`add_usd(load_cameras=True)`) and MJCF `<camera>` elements (`add_mjcf(parse_cameras=True)`).
  - Render model cameras or sites with `SensorCamera(model, cameras=...)` or `SensorCamera(model, sites=...)`, with view poses from the state, per-view near and far clipping, precomputed or analytic pinhole rays, and `set_camera_rays()` for custom lenses.
