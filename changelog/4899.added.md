Add model cameras:

  - Declare cameras with `ModelBuilder.add_camera()` and `newton.CameraLensType`, stored in `Model.camera_*` arrays with a `CAMERA` attribute frequency; cameras replicate per world and follow `collapse_fixed_joints()`.
  - Import cameras from USD `UsdGeom.Camera` prims (`add_usd(load_cameras=True)`) and MJCF `<camera>` elements (`add_mjcf(parse_cameras=True)`). MJCF tracking cameras are imported as fixed mounts and keep their `mode` and `target` in the `mujoco:camera_mode` (`SolverMuJoCo.CameraMode`) and `mujoco:camera_target` custom attributes.
  - Render model cameras or sites with the experimental bound sensor API, `SensorCamera(model, cameras=...)` or `SensorCamera(model, sites=...)`, with view poses from the state, per-view near and far clipping, rays projected from the Model's current camera intrinsics, and `set_camera_rays()` to give cameras or sites their own rays.
