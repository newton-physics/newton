Speed up the `propagation-colored` FeatherPGS contact solve by about 38% on dense single-world piles, and by 15-29% for colored sweeps at 128-1024 worlds. Contact rows with their friction pair, and lone contact rows, now take a straight-line path. The friction pair's operator setup is computed once per step, and few-world solves read each unit's rows from a packed per-step record. Long friction-patch rings are summed from a list instead of being walked. Results change only by floating-point summation order. Shared-memory staging is now opt-in, since the unstaged kernel matches or beats it.
