Guarantee that `SolverBase.step()` never writes `State.body_f` or `State.particle_f` in either state, so external forces written once persist across substeps and double-buffered states may share their force arrays. `SolverSemiImplicit` and `SolverFeatherstone` now accumulate internal forces in their own buffers instead of `state_in.particle_f` and `state_in.body_f`, and `SolverKamino` no longer copies the input wrenches to `state_out.body_f`. Code that read solver-internal forces back from these arrays should request solver observables instead.
