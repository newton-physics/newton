Guarantee that `SolverBase.step()` never writes `State.body_f` or `State.particle_f` in either state, so external forces written once persist across substeps and double-buffered states may share their force arrays. `SolverSemiImplicit` and `SolverFeatherstone` now accumulate internal forces in their own buffers instead of `state_in.particle_f` and `state_in.body_f`, and `SolverKamino`, `SolverCoupledProxy`, and `SolverCoupledADMM` no longer copy the input forces to `state_out`. No solver observable reports the elastic, contact, or joint forces that `SolverSemiImplicit` and `SolverFeatherstone` previously left in these arrays; code that read them must evaluate those forces itself. Code that relied on the forces being copied to `state_out` should share the force arrays between states or write the forces to each input state.
