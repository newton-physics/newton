Default `newton.usd.get_tetmesh()` and `TetMesh.create_from_usd()` to canonical-only material reads, completing the default migration announced in Newton 1.4: `compat_namespaces=None` now behaves like `()`, and `ModelBuilder.add_usd()` no longer reads vendor-namespaced TetMesh material attributes without a resolver. Stiffness and density are read only from a bound material that applies `PhysicsVolumeDeformableMaterialAPI` and authors canonical `physics:` attributes. `omniphysics:` / `physxDeformableBody:` attributes are ignored with a `NewtonWarning`, as are `physics:` attributes on a material without the API in `get_tetmesh()` and `create_from_usd()`; the mesh then has no material stiffness or density, and `add_usd()` uses the builder defaults. Author the canonical attributes with the material API, or keep the previous reads with `compat_namespaces=newton.usd.DEFORMABLE_LEGACY_NAMESPACES` (`get_tetmesh()` / `create_from_usd()`) or `schema_resolvers=[..., SchemaResolverPhysx()]` (`add_usd()`).
