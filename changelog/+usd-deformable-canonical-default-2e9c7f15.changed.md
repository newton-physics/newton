Default `newton.usd.get_tetmesh()` and `TetMesh.create_from_usd()` to canonical-only material reads, completing the default migration announced in Newton 1.4: `compat_namespaces=None` now behaves like `()`, and `ModelBuilder.add_usd()` no longer reads vendor-namespaced TetMesh material attributes without a resolver. `youngsModulus` and `poissonsRatio` are read only as `physics:` attributes from a bound material that applies `PhysicsVolumeDeformableMaterialAPI`, and `physics:density` also from one that applies `UsdPhysicsMaterialAPI`. `omniphysics:` / `physxDeformableBody:` attributes, and `physics:` attributes on a material without these APIs, are ignored: `get_tetmesh()` and `create_from_usd()` emit a `NewtonWarning` naming them when `compat_namespaces` is omitted, and `add_usd()` emits one naming the attributes it no longer reads from a material that authors vendor-namespaced attributes without `PhysicsVolumeDeformableMaterialAPI`. Author the canonical attributes with the material API, or keep the previous reads with `compat_namespaces=newton.usd.DEFORMABLE_LEGACY_NAMESPACES` (`get_tetmesh()` / `create_from_usd()`) or `schema_resolvers=[..., SchemaResolverPhysx()]` (`add_usd()`).
