Add the experimental `newton.solvers.SolverFeatherPGS`, a CUDA articulated-body solver that combines Featherstone dynamics with a matrix-free projected Gauss-Seidel solve for contacts with coupled Coulomb friction, optional joint limits (`enable_joint_limits`, off by default) and joint velocity limits, integrates joint drives implicitly, reports constraint-capacity overflow in `constraint_overflow` with the `(world_count + 1,)` layout of the reset mask (one entry per world and a final entry for global articulations), and supports world-masked `reset()` and CUDA graph capture. Known limitation: contacts between bodies of different worlds through a dynamic global body, or a kinematic global articulation with joints, cannot be solved; they are dropped and flagged, and the constructor warns when the model allows them. Unsupported model features, including enabled MuJoCo equality constraints that were not converted to Newton loop joints or mimic constraints (or whose projection link names an entity that does not join the row's bodies or joints), raise `NotImplementedError` instead of being ignored.
