Measure `SolverImplicitMPM.Config.critical_fraction` packing at each particle from the elastically deformed volumes of its neighbors, with a cubic B-spline of one voxel radius compared against the same kernel over the space outside colliders, instead of from strain-node particle volumes, and keep the packing bound nonnegative in every strain mode. Granular piles no longer settle into flat terraces; scenes that rely on `critical_fraction` may settle to slightly different packings.
