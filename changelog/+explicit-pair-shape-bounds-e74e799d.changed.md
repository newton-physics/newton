Speed up `CollisionPipeline.collide()` with `broad_phase="explicit"` (the default) for models without particles by updating shape bounds and narrow-phase shape data only for shapes in the pair list. In that case, `CollisionPipeline.geom_data`, `geom_transform`, `narrow_phase.shape_aabb_lower`, and `narrow_phase.shape_aabb_upper` are no longer updated for shapes outside the pair list, so do not read them for such shapes.
