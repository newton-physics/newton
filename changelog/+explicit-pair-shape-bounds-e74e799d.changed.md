Speed up `CollisionPipeline.collide()` with `broad_phase="explicit"` (the default) for models without particles and with a non-empty pair list by updating shape bounds and narrow-phase shape data only for shapes in that list. In that case, `CollisionPipeline.geom_data`, `geom_transform`, `narrow_phase.shape_aabb_lower`, and `narrow_phase.shape_aabb_upper` are no longer updated for shapes outside the pair list, so do not read them for such shapes.
