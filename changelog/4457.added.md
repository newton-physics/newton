Add experimental `SensorCamera.set_ambient_light()` to set the hemispheric ambient sky and ground colors, and an experimental `color` argument on `SensorCamera.create_default_light()`. Both are runtime inputs, so changing them does not recompile the render kernel. Default output is unchanged. How `SensorCamera` renders lights authored on the model, and how they combine with its own lights, is experimental in anticipation of importing lights from USD and MJCF.
