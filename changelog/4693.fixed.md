Make ``collision_filter_parent=False`` effective in ``SolverMuJoCo``: when a tree joint of the template world lets a parent-child shape pair collide (builder-authored joints, MJCF ``filterparent="disable"``, or USD joints with ``physics:collisionEnabled=true``), MuJoCo's native parent-child filter is disabled and the weld-group pairs of the joints that still filter are exported as excludes, so other assets keep their filtering. Previously such joints were silently ignored by MuJoCo.
