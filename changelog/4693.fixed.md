Make ``collision_filter_parent=False`` effective in ``SolverMuJoCo`` for non-weld joints: MuJoCo's native parent-child filter is always disabled and Newton's filter pairs are exported instead (as body excludes, or as per-slot masks for per-world mesh layouts), so a hinge, slider or other moving joint that lets its parent and child collide does so in MuJoCo as well. Previously such joints were silently ignored. Fixed and zero-DOF joints are welds: MuJoCo merges their bodies into one weld body whose geoms never collide with each other, whatever the joint's flag.
