Add sparse mass factors to `SolverFeatherPGS`: branched articulations that share one joint topology now assemble and factor their mass matrix in the fill-free pattern of the kinematic tree and solve their joint-limit and contact rows in the factor's coordinates; other articulations, models with mimic or loop-closing joints, and free bodies keep dense factors. The selection is structural, not a performance prediction. Add the opt-in `parallel_tree` option, which traverses the independent branches of each articulation tree in parallel.
