Fix `SolverVBD(rigid_contact_history=True)` restoring contacts from other contacts' multipliers and penalties when the match indices do not refer to the contact set the previous step solved: after a collision pass into another `Contacts` buffer, after two collision passes between steps, on a second step over the same contacts, and with a solver-owned pipeline under `PRE_POST_INIT` or when the rigid detection slot is toggled to `NONE`. Such contacts now start cold, a step over the same contacts restores each contact from its own state, and `PRE_POST_INIT` carries the restored state through its second pass.
