Change `SolverMuJoCo` to hold kinematic roots with a weld to a MuJoCo mocap target instead of a large internal armature. Kinematic roots keep their authored `joint_armature` and mass and no longer loosen solver convergence; contacts on a kinematic body use its real mass for their softness. Every movable root gets a mocap target and an inactive weld so `body_flags` can still switch it at runtime; pass `kinematic_switchable_roots=False` to prepare only roots that are kinematic when the solver is created. Making a non-root body kinematic now raises `ValueError`. Kinematic roots without mass or inertia, which MuJoCo cannot simulate as movable bodies, are driven as mocap bodies like fixed roots and cannot be switched to dynamic.
