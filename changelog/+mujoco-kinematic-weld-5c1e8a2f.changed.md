Change `SolverMuJoCo` to hold kinematic roots with a weld to a MuJoCo mocap target instead of a large internal armature. Kinematic roots keep their authored `joint_armature` and mass and no longer loosen solver convergence; contacts on a kinematic body use its real mass for their softness. Only roots that are kinematic in some world when the solver is created can be switched with `body_flags`; making any other root, or any non-root body, kinematic raises `ValueError`. Kinematic roots without mass or inertia, which MuJoCo cannot simulate as movable bodies, are driven as mocap bodies like fixed roots and cannot be switched to dynamic.
