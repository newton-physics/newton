Change `SolverMuJoCo` to hold kinematic roots with a weld to a MuJoCo mocap target instead of a large internal armature. Kinematic roots now keep their authored `joint_armature` and mass, have their own weight compensated while kinematic, solver convergence no longer depends on the locking armature, and contacts on a kinematic body use its real mass for their softness. Only roots that are kinematic in some world when the solver is created can be switched with `body_flags`; making any other root kinematic raises `ValueError`. Set the new `mujoco:kinematic_switchable` body attribute on roots that start dynamic but should become kinematic later, or pass `kinematic_switchable_roots=True` to make every root switchable. Making a non-root body kinematic at runtime now raises `ValueError` instead of being silently accepted.
