Change `SolverMuJoCo` to hold kinematic roots with a weld constraint instead of a large internal armature, so they keep their authored `joint_armature` and no longer loosen solver convergence. Making a non-root body kinematic, or making a root kinematic while MuJoCo's `equality` or `constraint` flag is disabled, now raises `ValueError`. Kinematic roots without mass or inertia, counting bodies attached by fixed joints, are now driven as MuJoCo mocap bodies that cannot be switched to dynamic. Mocap bodies have zero velocity in MuJoCo, so contact friction and dynamic descendants no longer see such a root's velocity, and a massless kinematic conveyor stops carrying objects; give the root mass and inertia to keep that coupling.
