Change `SolverMuJoCo` to hold kinematic roots with a weld to a MuJoCo mocap target instead of a large internal armature. Kinematic roots keep their authored `joint_armature` and mass and no longer loosen solver convergence; contacts on a kinematic body use its real mass for their softness. Only roots that are kinematic in some world when the solver is created can be switched with `body_flags`; making any other root, or any non-root body, kinematic raises `ValueError`. To start a root dynamic and make it kinematic later, mark it kinematic before creating the solver and switch it to dynamic afterwards. Kinematic roots without mass or inertia, which MuJoCo cannot simulate as movable bodies, are driven as mocap bodies like fixed roots and cannot be switched to dynamic. Mocap bodies posed from Newton joints, including fixed roots, now follow the model and state every step, also when `update_data_interval` skips pushing the joint state.
