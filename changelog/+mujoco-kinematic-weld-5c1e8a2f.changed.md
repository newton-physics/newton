Change `SolverMuJoCo` to hold kinematic roots with a weld to a MuJoCo mocap target instead of a large internal armature. Kinematic roots now keep their authored `joint_armature` and mass, solver convergence no longer depends on the locking armature, and contacts on a kinematic body use its real mass for their softness. Only roots that are kinematic in some world when the solver is created can be switched with `body_flags`; making any other root kinematic raises `ValueError`, so mark such bodies kinematic before constructing the solver.
