Add experimental point sets for particle-only deformables such as MPM granular and snow materials. `ModelBuilder.add_particle_grid()`, `ModelBuilder.add_particles(..., label=...)`, and USD simulation `Points` imports record `point_set_label` and `point_set_world` entries whose particle ranges follow `add_builder()` and `replicate()`. Finalized models expose `point_set_*` labels, worlds, counts, and particle ranges, and `newton.selection.DeformablePointSetView` reads and writes their particle state.
