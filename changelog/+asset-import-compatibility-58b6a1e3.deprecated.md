Deprecate the following import behavior in Newton 1.7:

  - The implicit URDF drive-damping default: `ModelBuilder.add_urdf(joint_damping_mode=None)` retains the Newton 1.6 drive mapping in 1.7 and emits `DeprecationWarning` only when the URDF authors `<dynamics damping>`. Pass `joint_damping_mode="drive"` to retain that interpretation or `joint_damping_mode="passive"` to opt into physical damping without a warning. The default will switch to `"passive"` in Newton 1.8; the explicit modes remain supported. The default transition will be handled in [Issue #4663](https://github.com/newton-physics/newton/issues/4663).
  - `ModelBuilder.add_usd(legacy_angular_velocity_units=True)`: enabling this temporary flag emits `DeprecationWarning`. It is scheduled for removal in Newton 1.8. Remove manual conversion of correctly authored joint-state velocities, or convert compensated radians/s values in degree-based attributes back to degrees/s, then disable the flag. Removal will be handled in [Issue #4664](https://github.com/newton-physics/newton/issues/4664).
  - `ModelBuilder.add_usd(legacy_springref_units=True)`: enabling this temporary flag emits `DeprecationWarning`. It is scheduled for removal in Newton 1.8. Author revolute `mjc:springref` in the units selected by `mjc:compiler:angle` (degrees by default), then disable the flag. Removal will be handled in [Issue #4664](https://github.com/newton-physics/newton/issues/4664).
