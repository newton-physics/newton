Support the `BODY_PARENT_F` and `CONTACT_F` solver observables in `SolverSteel`. `body_parent_f` is the joint wrench of the solved step, including contact, friction, spin and loop-closure loads, as `SolverMuJoCo` reports it. `contact_f` holds the linear force of each solved rigid contact (the torque part is zero); contacts without solved rows, such as rows dropped for capacity or skipped for sleeping islands, report zero.
