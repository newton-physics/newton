Add actuator motors, which turn the duty computed by the drive into motor effort: `newton.actuators.MotorDC`, a DC motor driven by average terminal voltage as in BAM (Better Actuator Models). PD, PID and neural drives take `output_space=DriveOutputSpace.DUTY` to compute a signed duty for a motor; a motor requires a duty drive and duty clamps, and an actuator without a motor rejects them. The actuator bounds duty to `[-1, 1]` and converts it using the actual supply voltage. Add `ClampingCurrentDuty`, which attempts current limiting from firmware estimates of the motor parameters, by default the motor's own `kt` and `resistance`, and the supplied or nominal voltage, then bounds duty to `[-max_duty, max_duty]`; it must be the only clamp of its actuator. Pass motors through `Actuator(motor=...)` or `ModelBuilder.add_actuator(motor=...)`; USD authors them with duty-configured drive schemas, `NewtonCurrentDutyClampingAPI` and `NewtonDCMotorAPI`. See the actuators concept documentation (`docs/concepts/actuators.rst`).
