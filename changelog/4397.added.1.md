Add `newton.actuators.DriveBAM`, the servo firmware and DC motor part of BAM (Better Actuator Models). With `supply_from_control=True` it reads the supply voltage per DOF from the `supply_voltage` custom input on `Control`, and uses `vin` where that voltage is not positive. USD actuator prims select `DriveBAM` with `NewtonBAMControlAPI` (`SchemaNames.BAM_CONTROL`).
