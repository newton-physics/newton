Add `newton.actuators.DriveBAM`, the servo firmware and DC motor part of BAM (Better Actuator Models), and `newton.actuators.Battery`, a supply shared by several actuators. `Battery.refresh(control)` sags each battery's voltage with the total `control.joint_f` effort on its DOFs and writes it into `control.supply_voltage`, which `DriveBAM(supply_from_control=True)` reads as a custom input. USD actuator prims select `DriveBAM` with `NewtonBAMControlAPI` (`SchemaNames.BAM_CONTROL`).
