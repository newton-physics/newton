Add `newton.actuators.DriveBAM`, the servo firmware and DC motor part of BAM (Better Actuator Models), and `newton.actuators.Battery`, a supply shared by several actuators. Pass the battery to `Actuator.step(..., battery=...)`; drives whose `uses_battery()` returns `True` read its voltage and add their motor torque, and the voltage sags with the total torque of all actuators on the battery, also across actuator groups.
