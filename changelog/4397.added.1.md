Add actuator motors, which turn the clamped effort into the effort of a motor as the last stage of an actuator: `newton.actuators.MotorBase` and `MotorDC`, a PWM-driven DC motor as in BAM (Better Actuator Models) that reproduces a BAM servo behind a `DrivePD`. Pass them through `Actuator(motor=...)` or `ModelBuilder.add_actuator(motor=...)`; USD authors the motor with `NewtonDCMotorAPI`.
