Add actuator motors, which turn the motor command computed by the drive into the effort of a motor as the last stage of an actuator: `newton.actuators.MotorDC`, a DC motor driven by a PWM voltage as in BAM (Better Actuator Models). Add `DriveVoltageControlled`, the firmware position controller of a BAM voltage-controlled servo, which outputs that voltage, and `ClampingCurrentPWM`, its current and PWM limits. With a motor, the actuator passes the supply voltage to the drive and the clampings through the new `supply_voltage` argument of `DriveBase.compute` and `ClampingBase.modify_forces`, and `ModelBuilder.add_actuator` passes the motor parameters named in the new `ClampingBase.MOTOR_PARAMS` to the clamping. Pass motors through `Actuator(motor=...)` or `ModelBuilder.add_actuator(motor=...)`; USD authors them with `NewtonVoltageControlAPI`, `NewtonCurrentPWMClampingAPI` and `NewtonDCMotorAPI`.
