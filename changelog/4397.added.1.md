Add actuator motors, which turn the motor command computed by the drive into the effort of a motor: `newton.actuators.MotorDC`, a DC motor driven by a PWM voltage as in BAM (Better Actuator Models). Add `DriveVoltageControlled`, the firmware position controller of a BAM voltage-controlled servo, which outputs that voltage, and `ClampingCurrentPWM`, its current and PWM limits; drives that output an effort, such as `DrivePD`, cannot be combined with a motor. Add `CommandClampingBase`, the base of clamps such as `ClampingCurrentPWM` that bound the motor command before the motor; the other clamps bound the effort of the motor after it. With a motor, the actuator passes the supply voltage to the drive and the command clamps through the new `supply_voltage` argument of `DriveBase.compute` and `CommandClampingBase.modify_forces`, and `ModelBuilder.add_actuator` passes the motor parameters named in `CommandClampingBase.MOTOR_PARAMS` to the command clamp. Pass motors through `Actuator(motor=...)` or `ModelBuilder.add_actuator(motor=...)`; USD authors them with `NewtonVoltageControlAPI`, `NewtonCurrentPWMClampingAPI` and `NewtonDCMotorAPI`. See the actuators concept documentation (`docs/concepts/actuators.rst`) for how the drive, clamps and motor of an actuator fit together.
