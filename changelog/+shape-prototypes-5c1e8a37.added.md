Add experimental shape prototypes and slots for per-world asset swaps: declare assets with `ModelBuilder.add_shape_prototype()` and replaceable shape rows with `ModelBuilder.add_shape_slot()`, switch them at runtime with `Model.assign_shape_prototypes()`, which also updates body mass properties, inspect them through the `Model.shape_prototype_*` and `Model.shape_slot_*` attributes, and propagate the change with the new `ModelFlags.SHAPE_GEOMETRY` flag and `ViewerBase.notify_model_changed()`. `ModelBuilder.shape_density` now records each shape's authored density.
