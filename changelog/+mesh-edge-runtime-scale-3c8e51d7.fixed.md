Fix mesh contacts on heightfield, mesh, and SDF shapes ignoring `Model.shape_scale` changes made after `ModelBuilder.finalize()` or after `CollisionPipeline` construction. Precomputed collision edges now apply the current shape scale. Texture SDFs built without a baked scale follow scale changes when the pipeline is constructed with `dynamic_shape_scale=True`; without it, rescaling such a shape that had unit scale at construction now raises a `RuntimeError` from a later `CollisionPipeline.collide()` call instead of being ignored.
