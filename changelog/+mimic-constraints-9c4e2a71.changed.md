Import MuJoCo joint equalities (MJCF `<equality><joint>` and USD `MjcEqualityJointAPI`) only as `model.mujoco.equality_constraint_*` rows; the importers no longer add a mimic constraint for them, their `equality_constraint_target_kind` is `0`, and `convert_mjc_equality_constraints` now only governs CONNECT and WELD equalities. `SolverSteel` enforces these rows directly when they couple two REVOLUTE or PRISMATIC joints of one articulation, reading their enabled flag and constant and linear `polycoef` terms every step, and warns about higher-order terms at construction instead of the importers warning at import. In `SolverMuJoCo` and `SolverSteel`, such an equality, enabled or not, takes the place of a mimic joint set on its `joint1`. Before, only an imported equality did, and `SolverMuJoCo` enforced one added with `newton.solvers.mujoco.add_equality_joint()` in addition to the mimic. Read the rows instead of `constraint_mimic_*` for imported equalities.
