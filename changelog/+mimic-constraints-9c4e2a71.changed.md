Import MuJoCo joint equalities (MJCF `<equality><joint>` and USD `MjcEqualityJointAPI`) only as `model.mujoco.equality_constraint_*` rows; the importers no longer add a mimic constraint for them, their `equality_constraint_target_kind` is `0`, and `convert_mjc_equality_constraints` now only governs CONNECT and WELD equalities. `SolverSteel` enforces these rows directly when they couple two REVOLUTE or PRISMATIC joints of one articulation, reading their enabled flag and constant and linear `polycoef` terms every step, and warns about higher-order terms at construction instead of the importers warning at import. A mimic joint set with `set_joint_mimic()` on the `joint1` of an imported joint equality is now enforced in addition to the equality in `SolverMuJoCo` and `SolverSteel`; before, the importers' mimic copy of the equality suppressed it, also while the equality was disabled. Keep only one of the two if their coefficients differ or the equality is disabled at runtime. Read the rows instead of `constraint_mimic_*` for imported equalities.
