Add solver-wide physics overrides to `newton.solvers.SolverFeatherPGS` whose defaults leave the model's properties in charge: `angular_damping` (one damping value for every free root, overriding the per-body `rigid_body_angular_damping` attribute; `None` by default), `enable_contact_friction` (`False` gives every contact a normal row only), `contact_friction_scale` (scales the shapes' friction coefficients in the solver's copy of the materials), `contact_friction_position_iterations` (solves tangential friction rows only in the last `k` position iterations; `-1`, the default, solves them in every iteration, and velocity-only iterations always solve them) and `enable_restitution` (`False` turns rebounds off regardless of the shapes' coefficients and lets the split solve and contact compliance accept shapes with positive restitution).
