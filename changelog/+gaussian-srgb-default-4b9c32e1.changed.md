Treat Gaussian spherical-harmonics radiance as sRGB by default and blend it in that encoding before decoding the result to linear light once per ray in `SensorCamera`, which also clamps negative radiance. `SensorCamera` output for untagged USD and PLY Gaussians is now darker. To keep the previous output, pass `sh_color_space=newton.utils.ColorSpace.LINEAR` to `newton.Gaussian`, or tag the USD radiance with the `lin_rec709_scene` color space. USD radiance in other color spaces, such as `lin_ap1_scene` or `acescg`, is imported without converting primaries or transfer function and logs a warning. The GL and Viser viewers honor `Gaussian.sh_color_space`, but the deprecated `SensorTiledCamera` ignores it.
