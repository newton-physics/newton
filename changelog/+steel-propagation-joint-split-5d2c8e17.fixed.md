Fix `SolverSteel` `propagation` and `propagation_colored` contacts dropping grasps when the contacting links share a moving ancestor but move mostly through their own joints, such as the pads of a linkage gripper on an arm. The sweep split each such link's whole response by the number of contacting links on the shared ancestor, which slowed friction convergence enough that grasps that `immediate` held slipped at the default iteration count. Only the shared joints' share of the response is split now.
