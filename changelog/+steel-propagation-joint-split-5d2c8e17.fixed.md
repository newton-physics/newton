Fix `SolverSteel` `propagation` and `propagation_colored` contacts dropping grasps when the contacting links share a moving ancestor but move mostly through their own joints, such as the pads of a linkage gripper on an arm. The sweep split each such link's whole response by the number of contacting links on the shared ancestor, which can slow friction convergence and allow grasps that `immediate` holds to slip at finite iteration counts. Only the shared joints' share of the response is split now.
