Convert legacy MuJoCo spring references once to Newton rest coordinates during finalization. Later `mujoco.dof_ref` edits leave `joint_rest_q` fixed and export `qpos_spring = joint_rest_q + ref`, replacing the behavior that kept the absolute MuJoCo spring reference fixed. To preserve an absolute equilibrium when changing `ref`, also update `joint_rest_q = springref - ref` and notify the solver with `ModelFlags.JOINT_REFERENCE_POSE_PROPERTIES`.
