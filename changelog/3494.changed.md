Make the contact damping of `SolverVBD` soft self-contact and rigid-soft contacts act while a contact closes and while it opens, with the matching Hessian; `soft_contact_damping_bidirectional=False` keeps closing-only damping. In both modes the self-contact damping rate is now the change of the contact distance over the substep, so a rigid motion of a contact pair gives no damping force, and closing-only self-contact damping can differ slightly from earlier releases for rotating or deforming pairs. Add `rigid_contact_damping_bidirectional` (default `False`) for the same two-sided damping of rigid-rigid contacts.
