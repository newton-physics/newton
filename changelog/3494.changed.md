Make the contact damping of `SolverVBD` soft self-contact and rigid-soft contacts act while a contact closes and while it opens, with the matching Hessian; `soft_contact_damping_bidirectional=False` restores the previous closing-only damping. The self-contact damping rate is the change of the contact distance over the substep, so a rigid motion of a contact pair gives no damping force. Add `rigid_contact_damping_bidirectional` (default `False`) for the same two-sided damping of rigid-rigid contacts.
