Compute rigid-body accelerations and parent-joint wrenches in `SolverMuJoCo` on the MuJoCo Warp backend with `disable_sensors=True`. Request `solver.ObservableKind.BODY_QDD` and `solver.ObservableKind.BODY_PARENT_F` through `solver.observables(kinds=...)` and pass the container to `step()`. Callers that previously handled a `ValueError` for these diagnostics with disabled sensors can now consume the computed outputs directly.
