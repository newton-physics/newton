Fix `SolverMuJoCo` applying actuator force to ball joints whose effort limit is zero. Ball-joint actuators are now always limited by `joint_effort_limit`, unless MJCF authors a `forcerange`; an authored `forcelimited="false"` alone no longer disables the limit. `save_to_mjcf` raises `ValueError` for an actuated ball joint with a zero effort limit, which MJCF cannot represent.
