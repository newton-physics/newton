Import the rigid bodies and joints of a USD stage as if the prims matched by `ModelBuilder.add_usd(ignore_paths=...)` were not on it, whether or not the pattern contains `.*`. A joint to a body ignored by a plain path is dropped instead of being attached to the world, and a body that an ignored body cuts off from its articulation is imported with its own base joint instead of being dropped. To keep the previous result, add the bodies that should be dropped to `ignore_paths` and pass `floating=False` or `base_joint` to attach the remaining bodies to the world. These options apply to every root body of the import and place the base joint at the body origin rather than at the dropped joint's anchor. Pass the temporary `legacy_ignore_paths=True` to retain the previous behavior while migrating.
