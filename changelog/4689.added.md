Support world-attached tendon guides with `body=-1` in XPBD and VBD, including
transformed builder composition, replication, and fixed-joint collapse into world.
Tendon guides no longer keep world-fixed bodies from collapsing; pass the relevant
fixed joints in `collapse_fixed_joints(joints_to_keep=...)` if their body frames
must remain, and do not reuse pre-collapse body indices.
