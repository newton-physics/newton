Read Rod angular rest in `SolverVBD` from `Model.joint_target_q` instead of `Model.body_q`; editing `body_q` before construction or notifying `BODY_PROPERTIES` no longer changes angular rest. To migrate, use `Rod(rest_points=..., rest_quaternions=...)`, `Rod(..., rest_straight=True)`, or `add_joint_rod(rest_rotation=...)`, and call `notify_model_changed(ModelFlags.JOINT_REFERENCE_POSE_PROPERTIES)` after editing `Model.joint_target_q`.
