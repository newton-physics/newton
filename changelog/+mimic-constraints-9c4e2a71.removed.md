Remove `ModelBuilder.add_constraint_mimic()`, the sparse `constraint_mimic_*` arrays of `Model` and `ModelBuilder`, and `Model.constraint_mimic_count`, deprecated in Newton 1.7, together with the names that only served them: `Model.AttributeFrequency.CONSTRAINT_MIMIC`, the `"constraint_mimic"` custom-attribute reference, and `SolverMuJoCo.mjc_eq_to_newton_mimic`. Replace each constraint with `builder.set_joint_mimic(follower_joint, reference_joint, coeffs=(offset, multiplier))` and read `Model.joint_mimic_joint` and `Model.joint_mimic_coeffs`; declare per-mimic custom attributes with `Model.AttributeFrequency.JOINT`, and read `SolverMuJoCo.mjc_eq_to_newton_joint_mimic` for the equality constraints of mimic joints. For a relationship that a mimic joint cannot express, such as a chain, several constraints on one follower, or one that is disabled at runtime, add a MuJoCo joint equality between two scalar joints with `newton.solvers.mujoco.add_equality_joint()`; `SolverMuJoCo` enforces it, and `SolverSteel` does when it couples two REVOLUTE or PRISMATIC joints of one articulation.
