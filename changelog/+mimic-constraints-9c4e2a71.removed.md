Remove `ModelBuilder.add_constraint_mimic()` and the sparse `constraint_mimic_*` arrays of `Model` and `ModelBuilder`, deprecated in Newton 1.7, together with `Model.constraint_mimic_count`, `Model.AttributeFrequency.CONSTRAINT_MIMIC`, the `"constraint_mimic"` custom-attribute reference, and `SolverMuJoCo.mjc_eq_to_newton_mimic`, which only served them. Replace each constraint with `builder.set_joint_mimic(follower_joint, reference_joint, coeffs=(offset, multiplier))` and read `Model.joint_mimic_joint` and `Model.joint_mimic_coeffs`. For a relationship that a mimic joint cannot express, such as a chain, several constraints on one follower, or one that is disabled at runtime, add a MuJoCo joint equality with `newton.solvers.mujoco.add_equality_joint()`; `SolverMuJoCo` and `SolverSteel` enforce it.
