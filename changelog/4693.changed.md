Filter parent-child collisions by weld group in ``ModelBuilder``: a joint with ``collision_filter_parent=True`` now filters the shapes of its child's weld group (the bodies joined by filtering fixed and zero-DOF tree joints) against its parent's weld group, and a filtering weld joint filters its whole group, as MuJoCo's own rule does. A weld with ``collision_filter_parent=False`` keeps its parent and child in separate groups. The pairs are completed in ``finalize()`` regardless of the order joints and shapes were added, and ``ShapeConfig.collision_filter_parent=False`` now keeps a shape out of the filter in either order. Models with fixed joints gain filter pairs across welded bodies; to keep two welded bodies colliding, add their joint with ``collision_filter_parent=False``, or their shapes with ``ShapeConfig(collision_filter_parent=False)``. ``finalize()`` now rejects fixed joints that close a cycle instead of looping.
