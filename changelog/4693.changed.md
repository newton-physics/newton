Filter parent-child collisions by weld group in ``ModelBuilder``: a joint with ``collision_filter_parent=True`` now filters the shapes of its child's weld group (the bodies joined by fixed and zero-DOF joints) against its parent's weld group, and a filtering weld joint filters its whole group, as MuJoCo's own rule does. The pairs are completed in ``finalize()`` regardless of the order joints and shapes were added, and ``ShapeConfig.collision_filter_parent=False`` now keeps a shape out of the filter in either order. Models with fixed joints gain filter pairs across welded bodies; to keep such bodies colliding, add their joint with ``collision_filter_parent=False`` or their shapes with ``ShapeConfig(collision_filter_parent=False)``.
