`SolverVBD` contact damping of soft self-contact and rigid-soft contacts now acts while a contact closes and while it opens, with the matching Hessian; the new option `soft_contact_damping_bidirectional=False` restores the previous closing-only damping. The new option `rigid_contact_damping_bidirectional` (default `False`) offers the same two-sided damping for rigid-rigid contacts.
