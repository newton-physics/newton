Apply previously ignored `Model.joint_friction` values in `SolverVBD` when
`rigid_compliant_alm=True`, including values from imported models. Set affected
friction values to zero to preserve the previous friction-free behavior.
When VBD integrates rigid bodies, require finite bounds and reject positive
friction on either joint of a supported mimic pair, including disabled joints.
For D6 joints with positive friction, also require at most three axes per axis
group and require each group to be orthonormal. Finite nonpositive values
disable friction. Invalid configurations raise `ValueError` at solver
construction and when
`SolverVBD.notify_model_changed(ModelFlags.JOINT_DOF_PROPERTIES)` is called.
