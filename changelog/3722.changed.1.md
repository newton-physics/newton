Read the four structural `physics:curves*Stiffness` cable material attributes and resolve each unauthored mode from `physics:youngsModulus`, `physics:poissonsRatio`, and simulation-geometry `physics:thicknesses` instead of using `add_rod()` defaults. An unauthored thickness uses AOUSD's 1 mm diameter fallback (0.5 mm radius); short segments at this diameter may have their inertia corrected during model finalization. To migrate an existing material-owned `physics:curvesThickness`, move the value to `physics:thicknesses` on the simulation geometry and author the matching `physics:thicknesses:elementType` (`constant`, `curve`, `segment`, or `point`).
