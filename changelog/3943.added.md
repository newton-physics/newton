Add `CollisionPipeline(soft_contact_matching=True)` to anchor rigid-soft contacts across collision passes, independently of `contact_matching`. Each record keeps an anchor, its earlier soft-side and body-side points, while they touch, have slipped apart by at most `contact_matching_pos_threshold`, and lie on a surface without surface velocity, with a body-frame normal on the same side as before, so contact moving to the opposite face of a thin wall starts over. `SolverVBD` solves anchored records at their anchors, the fresh contact points in `Contacts` stay unchanged, and only buffers used with such a pipeline allocate anchors. Its `CONTACT_F` rows report the rigid-side wrench at the private anchors; reconstructing particle forces or application points from the fresh `Contacts` geometry is unsupported in this mode. Run one collision pass with an externally constructed buffer before CUDA graph capture; buffers from the pipeline's `contacts()` are prepared automatically.
