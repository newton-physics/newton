Add batteries shared by several actuators: `ModelBuilder.add_battery()` takes a battery class, e.g. `newton.actuators.BatteryInternalResistance`, and its parameters, and `ModelBuilder.finalize()` creates one `newton.actuators.BatteryBase` object per class in `Model.batteries`, whose `step()` and `reset()` update the supply voltage of its actuators. Batteries are authored in USD with a `NewtonBattery` prim and a `newton:battery` relationship on `NewtonActuator`, which `newton.actuators.parse_battery_prim()` and `ActuatorParsed.battery_path` expose.
