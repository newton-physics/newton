Add batteries shared by several actuators. `ModelBuilder.add_battery(joints, *, nominal_voltage, sag_gain, min_voltage)` adds a battery for the given joints of the current world, by index or label, and stores its parameters on the `Model` under the `actuator` namespace, so `replicate()` copies them per world. `newton.actuators.update_battery_supply(model, control, *, effort=None)` sags each battery's voltage with the total effort on its DOFs, `control.joint_f` by default, and writes it into `control.supply_voltage`; `newton.actuators.reset_battery_supply(model, control, *, world_mask=None)` restores the nominal voltage. USD `NewtonBattery` prims (`SchemaNames.BATTERY`) are imported with `ModelBuilder.add_battery()`, and the `DriveBAM` actuators of their joints read the battery voltage.
