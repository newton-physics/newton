Add batteries shared by the motors of several actuators: `ModelBuilder.add_battery()` takes a battery class, e.g. `newton.actuators.BatteryConstantResistance`, and its parameters, and `ModelBuilder.finalize()` creates one `newton.actuators.BatteryBase` object per class in `Model.batteries`. `BatteryBase.step(state_in, state_out)` turns the current the motors draw, `state_in.actuator_supply_current`, into their supply voltage, `state_out.actuator_supply_voltage`, and `BatteryBase.reset(state)` restores the voltage. Batteries are authored in USD with a prim derived from `NewtonBattery`, e.g. `NewtonConstantResistanceBattery`, and a `newton:battery` relationship on `NewtonActuator`, which `newton.actuators.parse_battery_prim()`, returning a `BatteryParsed`, and `ActuatorParsed.battery_path` expose.
