Deprecate `ArticulationView.get_actuator_parameter(actuator, component, name)` and `ArticulationView.set_actuator_parameter(actuator, component, name, values, mask=None)`. Use `get_actuator_parameter(name, component)` and `set_actuator_parameter(name, component, values, mask=mask)` instead, and reshape values from `(world, articulation * DOF)` to `(world, articulation, DOF)`.
