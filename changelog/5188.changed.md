Read `mjc:inertia` (`"legacy"`, `"exact"`, `"convex"`, or `"shell"`) via `SchemaResolverMjc` during USD import, defaulting mesh colliders with `MjcMeshCollisionAPI` applied to `"legacy"` to match MuJoCo while leaving other USD meshes unchanged. To keep exact signed-volume inertia on an `MjcMeshCollisionAPI` prim, author `mjc:inertia = "exact"` or omit `SchemaResolverMjc` from `schema_resolvers`.
