Add contact force reporting to `SolverVBD`: allocate with `solver.observables(kinds={solver.ObservableKind.CONTACT_F})` and pass the container to `step()` to populate `observables.contact_f` with one wrench per body-body contact (when VBD integrates the rigid bodies) and per rigid-soft particle, edge, or face contact record. Each wrench gives the force on body 0 or the contacted shape's body and torque about its center of mass, evaluated at the final configuration of the step. Reporting supports substep selection, graph capture, and `SensorContact` (rigid contacts only).
