Add `ClampingSymmetric(limit, input_space=...)` for effort or duty bounds. PD, PID and neural drives accept `output_space=DriveOutputSpace.EFFORT` or `DUTY`; motor configurations reject effort drives/clamps, and motor-free configurations reject duty components. The actuator always bounds duty to `[-1, 1]`. `ClampingCurrentDuty` uses independently authored firmware parameters `kt_estimate` and `resistance_estimate`, without copying the motor’s physical parameters.
