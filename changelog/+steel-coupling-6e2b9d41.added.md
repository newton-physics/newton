Let the experimental `SolverSteel` own rigid bodies and articulations in the experimental coupled solvers, as a `SolverCoupledProxy` source or a `SolverCoupled`/`SolverCoupledADMM` entry: it reports articulated effective mass and inertia for coupling endpoints, and keeps its friction-patch history across coupler iteration restarts. Owning particles, sleeping, contact warm starting, contact compliance and contact torsion raise `NotImplementedError` when the coupled solver is constructed, and so does `update_mass_matrix_interval` above 1 under a coupler with more than one iteration. Acting as a proxy destination raises `NotImplementedError` on the first coupled step.
