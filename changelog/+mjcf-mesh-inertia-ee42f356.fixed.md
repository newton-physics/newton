Honor the MJCF mesh `inertia` attribute (`legacy`, `exact`, `convex`, or `shell`) when computing the mass properties of imported mesh geoms, matching native MuJoCo, including its convex hull for an authored `maxhullvert`, picked before the mesh's scale and reference pose. A mesh without faces now takes its mass from MuJoCo's convex hull of all its vertices instead of Newton's collision hull, also with `inertia="exact"`. An `inertia="exact"` mesh with inconsistently oriented faces, which MuJoCo rejects, now logs a warning and keeps Newton's exact mass properties.
