Add actuators that read or drive several joints. `newton.actuators.StateEncoderBase` and `StateEncoderFixed` measure the position and velocity the drive reads as weighted sums of the input joints, e.g. behind a backlash joint with `position_gear=[1, 1]`; USD authors it with `NewtonActuatorEncoderAPI` and `newton:outputJoint`. `newton.actuators.TransmissionBase` and `TransmissionFixed` map the actuator effort onto several output joints with a gear ratio per joint, and give clamps and the motor the motor velocity through the same ratios. Pass them through `Actuator(encoder=..., transmission=...)` or `ModelBuilder.add_actuator(index=[...], effort_index=[...], encoder=(StateEncoderFixed, {"position_gear": [...]}), transmission=(TransmissionFixed, {"gear": [...]}))`. Add `newton.actuators.ActuatorBase`, the interface `Actuator` implements; `Model.actuators` is typed as a list of it.
