Add transmissions, which let an actuator drive several joints: `newton.actuators.TransmissionBase`, `TransmissionDirect` (the default) and `TransmissionFixed`. `TransmissionFixed` maps the joints of an actuator onto one actuator coordinate with a gear ratio per joint, e.g. the mean finger position `0.5 qL + 0.5 qR` of a gripper, and maps the actuator effort back onto them with the same ratios. The input processors, drive, clamps and motor work in this coordinate. Actuators with a transmission other than `TransmissionDirect` read their targets and feedforward per actuator, from `Control.actuator_target_pos`, `actuator_target_vel` and `actuator_feedforward`. Pass them through `Actuator(transmission=..., actuator_indices=...)` or `ModelBuilder.add_actuator(index=[...], transmission=(TransmissionFixed, {"gear": [...]}))`; `ModelBuilder.add_actuator()` also takes the new `effort_index` to write the effort to other DOFs.
