Add transmissions, which let an actuator drive several joints: `newton.actuators.TransmissionBase` and `TransmissionFixed` map the joints of an actuator onto one actuator coordinate with a gear ratio per joint, e.g. a gripper opening `0.5 qL + 0.5 qR`, and map the actuator effort back onto them with the same ratios. The input processors, drive, clamps and motor work in this coordinate, and `TransmissionDirect`, the default, makes the actuator its single joint. Actuators with a transmission read their targets and feedforward per actuator, from `Control.actuator_target_pos`, `actuator_target_vel` and `actuator_feedforward`. Pass them through `Actuator(transmission=...)` or `ModelBuilder.add_actuator(index=[...], transmission=(TransmissionFixed, {"gear": [...]}))`. Add `newton.actuators.ActuatorBase`, the interface `Actuator` implements; `Model.actuators` is typed as a list of it.
