- Warn when a non-convex `MESH` collider is exported to the MuJoCo solver, which compiles every mesh geom as a convex hull — non-convex cavities are not simulated and bodies may rest on the hull surface. The warning fires only when MuJoCo generates the contacts; meshes too large to verify convexity get a softer unverified-convexity warning instead of silently hulling. Approximate concave meshes with `ModelBuilder.approximate_meshes("coacd")` or set `use_mujoco_contacts=False` to keep Newton's exact-triangle collisions. (#4065)
