- Warn when a non-convex `MESH` collider is exported to the MuJoCo solver, which compiles every mesh geom as a convex hull — non-convex cavities are not simulated and bodies may rest on the hull surface. The warning fires only when MuJoCo generates the contacts and only when the convex hull closes over a meaningful share of extra volume (≥ 5%), so near-convex link meshes (e.g. MuJoCo Menagerie robot assets, where hulling is the authored intent) stay silent. Meshes too large to verify convexity get a softer unverified-convexity warning instead of silently hulling. Both warnings use dedicated categories (`newton.geometry.NonConvexMeshWarning` and `newton.geometry.UnverifiedConvexityWarning`) so they can be filtered cleanly without message regexes. Approximate concave meshes with `ModelBuilder.approximate_meshes("coacd")` or set `use_mujoco_contacts=False` to keep Newton's exact-triangle collisions. (#4065)
