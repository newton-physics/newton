Add an experimental `SolverVBD(rigid_rod_direct_solve=True)` option that solves each rod (a chain, tree or loop of bodies joined by `JointType.ROD` joints) with one direct Newton step per iteration instead of per-body block descent, so slender rods whose stretch stiffness far exceeds their bending stiffness bend and carry axial load in a few iterations. It also allows different bending stiffness about the two bend axes, and adds `SolverVBD.check_rod_direct_status()` to report refused steps.
