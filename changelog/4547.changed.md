Read REVOLUTE `State.joint_q` as persistent winding state in `SolverSemiImplicit`, and update only REVOLUTE entries of output `joint_q`/`joint_qd`. Migrate body-only initialization, resets, and copies to also set the corresponding REVOLUTE coordinates; stale winding can add whole turns to drive and limit errors. Set joint coordinates then call `eval_fk`, or call `eval_ik` after a body-pose reset when discarding winding is intended. For standalone and loop-closing joints outside articulation metadata, reset body poses and REVOLUTE coordinates directly; `eval_fk` and `eval_ik` do not update them. Use separate `eval_ik` output arrays for read-out during simulation. Other joint coordinates remain unrefreshed, and `eval_ik` overwrites multi-turn winding with wrapped angles.
