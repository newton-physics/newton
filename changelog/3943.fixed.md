Fix `SolverVBD` body-particle friction creeping under any static tangential load, such as cloth sliding out of a friction pinch, when contacts come from `CollisionPipeline(contact_matching="sticky")`: friction now acts on the drift between the persistent contact points instead of the motion since the last collision pass, and its cone is the normal load each contact carried at the end of the previous step, elastic and damping together, so `soft_contact_damping_bidirectional` no longer changes how far a sliding contact travels.
