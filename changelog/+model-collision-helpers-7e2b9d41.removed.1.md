Remove the `soft_contact_margin` argument of `CollisionPipeline` and `CollisionPipeline.collide()`, the `CollisionPipeline.soft_contact_margin` attribute, and `CollisionPipeline.soft_rigid_contact_pair_count`, deprecated in Newton 1.6. Pass `soft_contact_gap` to the constructor or `set_collision_detection_range()`. The setter changes the gap for subsequent calls. To replace a one-call override, save `pipeline.soft_contact_gap` before setting it and restore the saved value after `collide()`. Read `soft_contact_gap` and `soft_contact_pair_count` in place of the old attributes. Assigning `soft_contact_margin` now raises `AttributeError`.
